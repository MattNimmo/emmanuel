**TLDR:** Original KB videos cost the same as Sunday bumpers (8–12 hrs). The real savings come from KB re-edits (1–4 hrs), not from KB vs. Sunday as a category distinction.

---

## Time Cost Comparison

| Video Type | Hours Avg | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday Bumper | 8–12 hrs | Up to 30 hrs for highly unique productions |
| KB Promo/Announcement (original content) | 8–12 hrs | Mapped to Bumper Video Production reference |
| KB Re-edit / Re-brand (partner or trip content) | 1–4 hrs | When working from existing footage |

---

## Key Finding

The wiki estimates KB original videos using **Bumper Video Production** as the reference — there is no separate lower estimate for KB originals. The meaningful cost variable is **original vs. re-edit**, not **KB vs. Sunday**.

- Original KB content = same cost as a Sunday bumper (8–12 hrs)
- KB re-edits = 4–8× cheaper (1–4 hrs)
