## 2025 Reference — Key Overlap Patterns

Before projecting forward, three lessons from 2025 inform the risk map:

1. **November is the hardest month in the year**, confirmed by both 2024 and 2025. KB Series + Miracle Offering + Sisterhood Fall + KB Weekend #2 all land simultaneously, and Christmas design must begin before November ends.
2. **August is a false calm.** The team is in summer series mode, but Fall Kickoff prep and the CG Campaign booklet both need to be underway. This is historically the most underplanned month.
3. **January runs four concurrent tracks:** New year series, 21 Days of Prayer & Fasting (not on master calendar but a real recurring project), Vision Sunday annual report booklet, and KB Catalytic Weekend #1. The annual report booklet must be designed in December while Christmas is still live.
