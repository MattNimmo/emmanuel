**TLDR:** ECC is in the most consequential organizational moment since Lakeville launched. Two things are happening at once: a summer creative season with genuine momentum (Seven Churches of Revelation, Holy Spirit Night), and a leadership restructuring that Nate Ruch openly described as years overdue. The May 20 EOS meeting marks a before/after line for how the organization understands itself.

---

## The Organizational Turn

The May 20 meeting was not a planning session. It was a disclosure.

Nate Ruch opened by telling his entire directional leadership team — location pastors, central ministry directors, JC, Josh Patterson, Matt Nimmo, and others — something he'd been carrying privately for years:

> "I am both lead pastor and executive pastor right now, and that's not sustainable. The church is not getting the best version of me right now."
> "I don't want to go another year the way the last five have been. It's taken a toll on my body."

When former Executive Pastor Ben left, Nate absorbed both roles. The DLT adapted by each wearing "two hats" — a ministry area plus a whole-church hat with Nate. But no one was functioning as a traditional number two. Decision-making slowed. Accountability became murky. Nate's capacity for vision work, the one thing only he can give the church, eroded.

He's turning 55 this summer. He named it plainly: he's entering a 10-year window to 65, and succession planning at some point in his sixties is simply logical. The restructuring isn't crisis management — it's pre-positioning.

**What he's doing about it:**
1. Formalizing an organizational operating system ("Emmanuel Operating System," based on EOS) with board member Andy Ballew as consultant. VTO and accountability chart development begin now. Target: all six EOS components operational by ~November 2026.
2. Accelerating the Executive Pastor search. Budget being built for a 2027 hire. Looking for an "integrator" — someone operationally minded who can run the system underneath a visionary lead pastor.

Andy Ballew's diagnostic named the problems without softening them: firefighter culture, thin bench strength, clunky systems, inconsistent location experience, unclear central-vs.-location accountability, and a passive-aggressive water-cooler dynamic that suppresses honest feedback upward. JonCarlos Velez's line landed hardest:

> "We want an off-road vehicle but continue to drive a sports car."

And his follow-up: "We function more as family rather than a team." At family level, dysfunction can coexist with love indefinitely. At team level, it can't.

**What this means going forward:**
- The accountability chart will clarify who owns what — including work that currently falls into the gap between "I thought they had it" and "I thought you had it."
- An XP arriving in 2027 will become the primary route for staff issues, direction, and permissions that currently flow to Nate directly. Staff who are here because of direct access to Nate will experience the biggest shift.
- Core values are being redefined. The current wall letters are not formal values. "Joy" and "fun" will likely come off the list. Something more behaviorally specific will replace them.
- Nate explicitly said: don't stop anything currently in motion. No StratOPS-style pause. The EOS work is additive.

The comms and creative teams are not yet in the EOS build process, but it will cascade to department-level VTOs. Expect a Comms VTO and a Creative VTO to be asked for once the org-level vision work settles.

## The Summer Creative Moment

While the organizational work unfolds, the creative season ahead has more clarity and energy than usual.

**May 24 — Bill Johnson (What About? #5)**
Guest speaker. Final entry in the "What About?" series. Memorial Day mid-service moment. Holy Spirit Night childcare registration CTA in central announcements.

**May 31 — Holy Spirit Night Sunday**
Pastor Nate preaching. The Sunday service is the on-ramp to HSN that evening. Grad honoring moment added (new tradition: Sunday after Memorial Day, owned by Next Gen). This is the last Sunday before Seven Churches begins.

**June 7 → late July — Seven Churches of Revelation**
The creative direction is locked. It came out of Nate sharing his Turkey trip in a May 20 brainstorm session with JC, Matt Nimmo, Zac Anderson, and Sara Wescott. The series has something most ECC series don't: firsthand source material the senior pastor personally experienced.

The frame: ancient letters to seven real churches, in cities Nate walked through, with an archaeologist-guide (Jeremy) whose teachings Nate has on audio. The Laodicea story alone — a hidden house church discovered under the city, proof that the "lukewarm" church repented within 50 years of receiving the letter — is the kind of detail that makes a series feel earned rather than assembled.

**Creative direction confirmed:**
- Proposed subtitle: "The Future Is Ancient"
- Platform: white drapes scrunched into column shapes, uplit, with gobo projections of Turkey imagery
- Lobby: photo backdrop at each location (Ephesus Library arch, prop columns, palm trees) — a photo moment that anchors attendees in the series all summer
- Promo video: narrative voiceover + Turkey B-roll + Jeremy's guide audio; possible Anoka amphitheater B-roll shoot; style cue: "this is our history"
- Tour poster concept: series branded like a rock tour schedule listing upcoming "cities"
- Social: after-service "What's your takeaway today?" interviews, 20 seconds, diverse ages; run weekly mid-week

This is not a generic summer series. The archaeology gives it grounding. The "ancient-present collision" aesthetic (JC's phrase) is genuinely differentiated.

## Infrastructure Building Underway

Below the strategic and creative layers, the operational baseline is tightening:

- **Teams script channel launched** — Matt Nimmo introduced a centralized service asset repository in Teams (service prep + scripts folder). This is exactly the kind of standardized process the EOS work is trying to scale; it arrived organically before EOS formally started.
- **Alert comms protocol tightened** — after a camera controller incident during May 17 service (Zac pushed settings to the live camera), the rule is now explicit: all technical alerts go to PC Alive, not the Sunday chat, so the full production team (including volunteers) sees them.
- **Confidence monitor failure (SLP)** — countdown monitor glitched all morning May 17. Tech appointment scheduled. A recurring category of issue, not a one-off.
- **Next Gen gap named** — Pastor Manny's absence from weekly planning meetings has been causing misses (grad moment nearly fell through). Named as a structural gap; Manny should be in or briefed after weekly planning.
- **15 weeks of service times data** — dashboard is current through May 17. MG and LV service totals now corrected for phantom songs. SLP and LV timer issues from prior weeks resolved.

## Tensions Worth Tracking

**Organizational trust deficit.** Nate acknowledged it publicly: "People don't feel trusted by me." He also named the end-around culture (staff going to Nate directly when they disagree with their supervisor) as something that must stop when an XP arrives. These are years-deep patterns. The EOS framework addresses structure; the culture part is harder and will take longer.

**Comms team + EOS timing.** The comms team is already at capacity (85% threshold flagged in April). EOS implementation will generate new deliverables — core values rollout, VTO communication, XP announcement, culture change messaging. None of that is in the current capacity model. The team should be consulted early about what EOS asks of comms.

**August series TBD.** Seven Churches runs through late July. August planning wasn't locked in the May 20 session. If the series extends into broader Revelation or if Ephesians starts early, that affects bumper video and series graphic production timelines. This decision needs to be made by early June to stay ahead of Sara's capacity.

**Seven Churches creative execution window.** June 7 is the first Sunday. Platform work (drape treatment), lobby backdrops at four locations, promo video, and tour poster all need to be done in the next ~2–3 weeks. This is a tight window, especially for Sara (sole designer). The Anoka amphitheater shoot is an additional variable.

**XP culture transition.** When an XP arrives in 2027, the relational dynamics of the organization will change. Staff who currently have direct access to Nate will need to route through the XP for operational matters. Nate named this explicitly; it will require pastoral communication, not just an announcement.
