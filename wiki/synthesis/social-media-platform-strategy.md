**TLDR:** ECC currently posts ~3–4 times/week across all platforms combined, with no strategy, no owner, and no platform differentiation. This page proposes a two-phase approach: Phase 1 gets to ~8–10 posts/week using existing team capacity with better systems; Phase 2 is the One Church Columbus volunteer room model with 15+ posts/week at scale.

---

## Current State Baseline

| Platform | Current Frequency | Current Owner | Problem |
|----------|------------------|---------------|---------|
| Instagram / Facebook | 2–3 posts/week | Sara | Bulletin board only; no Reels; no strategy |
| YouTube | 1 full message/week | Tanner | No Shorts; no Premiere strategy |
| Email | 1/week | Zac | ✅ Working — keep as-is |
| Pando | Operational only | Tanner | Uptime, not social |
| App | Sporadic | Sara (absorbed) | No real social function |

**Total: ~3–4 posts/week.** Almost zero growth-driving content. No Reels. No Shorts. No real-time Sunday presence.

---

## Phase 1 — Now (Current Team, Better Systems)

**Goal:** Squeeze more reach out of content the team is already producing, without adding significant new work. Most of this content already exists — it just isn't being formatted and distributed systematically.

### Target Frequency

| Platform | Target | Format Mix | Owner |
|----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Instagram | 5x/week | 2 Reels, 2 static/carousel, 1 Story set | Sara |
| Facebook | 4x/week | Mirror Instagram posts; skip Stories | Sara (scheduled/automated) |
| YouTube | 2x/week | 1 full Sunday message + 1 Short from sermon clips | Tanner |
| Email | 1x/week | Keep current | Zac |

**Total: ~12 posts/week** (vs. ~4 today), using content that mostly already exists.

### How Phase 1 Gets There Without Burning More Hours

1. **Reels from sermon clips** — Tanner already cuts sermon clips weekly. The same cut, formatted vertically with captions, becomes a Reel. This is a format conversion, not new production.
2. **YouTube Shorts** — same clip, different platform. One edit, two distributions.
3. **Batch scheduling** — Sara schedules a full week of Instagram posts in one session rather than daily one-offs. Tools: Later, Buffer, or Meta Business Suite (free).
4. **Story templates** — a standard weekly Story set (Sunday preview Friday, Sunday recap Monday) built once in Canva; Sara swaps the image and posts. ~15 min/week.
5. **Facebook automation** — Instagram posts auto-publish to Facebook via Meta Business Suite. No extra effort.

### What Phase 1 Does Not Require
- A new hire
- A volunteer room
- A platform strategy for every channel
- Approval from anyone except JC for content direction

### Phase 1 Constraint
Sara is the single point of failure. If she's in a crunch week (P0 season, major event), social drops. That's acceptable in Phase 1 — the priority framework already accounts for this. Social is P1, not P0.

## Phase 2 — Vision (Volunteer Room Model)

**Goal:** One Church Columbus model. A dedicated social room, 10+ volunteers, game plan board, real-time Sunday posting across multiple channels. Social becomes a growth engine, not a distribution afterthought.

### Target Frequency

| Platform | Target | Format Mix | Owner |
|----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Instagram | 10–14x/week | Reels daily, Stories every day, behind-the-scenes, testimonies | Volunteer social lead + Sara |
| Facebook | 7x/week | Mirrored from Instagram | Automated |
| YouTube | 3x/week | Full message, 1–2 Shorts, 1 Worship clip | Tanner + volunteers |
| TikTok | 3–5x/week | Reels repurposed; vertical-native content | Volunteer lead |
| Email | 1x/week | Keep current | Zac |

**Total: ~25–35 posts/week** across all channels, including real-time Sunday content.

### What Phase 2 Requires

1. **A galvanizing leader** — not a staff FTE; a volunteer (ideally young, content-native) who runs the room and owns the game plan board. JC's framing: "younger face, someone that can help galvanize people; you gotta be people on the YouTube."
2. **A physical space** — corner of the shoot studio, 2–3 laptops, charging stations, devices from IT. JC on Sara: "Give her a space, and she'd be pumped."
3. **A Sunday game plan board** — one per platform, mapped out Thursday before each Sunday. Specifies what gets posted, when, who owns each post.
4. **Sara's visit to One Church Columbus** — planned for June/July 2026. She goes on a Sunday, observes the room in action, brings back the playbook.
5. **A platform strategy doc** — what each platform is *for*, who the audience is, what the CTA is. Does not exist yet (see open questions below).

## The 180 Connection

The [[180-testimony-videos]] strategy is the content engine that feeds Phase 2. Quick-tier 180s (1–2 min) are purpose-built for Instagram Reels and TikTok. Mini-docs are YouTube. Without a testimony pipeline, Phase 2 is still bulletin-board content at higher volume — which is just more noise.

**The sequence matters:** Phase 1 builds the distribution habit. 180s build the content library. Phase 2 is what happens when both are running.

## Open Questions (Decisions Needed)

1. **Who is the volunteer social lead?** This is the Phase 2 unlock. Until this person exists, Phase 2 stays in vision mode.
2. **What does TikTok require before ECC is on it?** Content policy, brand voice, leadership buy-in — not there yet.
3. **Does Zach Brauer run ELC/youth social as a contractor?** Sara has reservations; Matt and JC evaluating. If yes, this frees Sara's Phase 1 capacity significantly.
4. **What is Sara's weekly social budget in hours?** Phase 1 only works if her social work doesn't expand to fill the capacity freed elsewhere. Needs a ceiling.
5. **Does Jodi own any part of online/social strategy?** She bridges Pando and social; she's an implementer. If she owns a slice of online social distribution, that helps Phase 1 without adding headcount.
