## 2. Themes

### TEC 2026 is now the primary active build
Seven Churches consumed all of April–May's creative attention. As of June 1, TEC (Aug 15) is the successor — and it's legitimately complex. Three things are new this cycle:
- **Essential Leadership Track** — authored by Jodi Ruch + Brandi Hammond; being launched publicly for the first time at TEC 2026. Nathan Grams is the primary voice. This is not a minor agenda item — it's the afternoon content architecture for the whole conference.
- **77 pre-registrants ahead of pace** — strong signal that the June 2 Champions meeting and personal invite strategy may have already moved people.
- **Known gaps: decor lead open, young adult attendance unresolved, communication timeline undefined.** None of these close themselves. See [[tec-planning-meeting-06-01]].

### Leadership pipeline codified for the first time
ECC has had Growth Track as an on-ramp since at least 2025 TEC. Before this week, nothing beyond that was named publicly. Jodi and Brandi's 4-tier framework (Team Member / Leader / Coach / Coordinator) is the first explicit development ladder in the org. The math scales (1 Coordinator can umbrella 250 people), but the training infrastructure for Intermediate and Advanced levels doesn't exist yet. This is vision ahead of system — which is exactly the territory Matt's wired to build into. See [[leadership-pipeline]] and [[leadership-levels-playbook]].

### Service times: escalating overruns with no corrective action
May 31 was the worst service data in the dataset:
- **MG Close Worship 11am: 4:35 vs 0:45 plan (+3:50). New dataset-worst.**
- **SLP: +12:59 at 9am / +14:56 at 11am.** Grad Sunday + Holy Spirit Night announcement drove LV mid to +6:05 — its biggest mid in the dataset.
- Messages ran 45:20–46:21 all campuses vs 38-min plan.

May 24 also showed MG Close Worship resurging hard (+2:16/+1:39) after a one-week dip. The pattern isn't noise anymore — it's structural, and it's worsening. The data is conclusive enough that the question isn't *whether* to fix MG Close Worship, it's *who owns moving the plan.* That's currently unanswered. See [[service-times-tracking]].

### Gravity Engine is at beta
From the June 1 ingest: Luke Fredrickson set internal beta at ~1–2 weeks from May 28. That's this week. No update in the wiki. If the beta is live, it changes the website strategy picture — [[website-strategy]] was already flagged as superseded by the custom build. A post-beta check-in would tell us whether the WordPress vendor evaluation is fully dead or still running in parallel. See [[gravity-engine-meeting-05-28]].

### Seven Churches launched — no debrief yet
The entire May creative arc pointed at a June 7 launch. It launched. The wiki has no post-launch entry: no service debrief, no reflection on whether the aesthetic executed (drape columns, lobby backdrops, Turkey B-roll), no sense of how the team experienced the transition from "building it" to "running it." That's the kind of signal the wiki should capture while it's fresh — but it won't unless something gets ingested. See [[seven-churches-of-revelation]].
