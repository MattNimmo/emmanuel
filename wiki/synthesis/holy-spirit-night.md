## Teaching Approach (Updated May 18)

Source: [[message-planning-meeting-05-04]]

- **Length:** 15–25 min teaching at Holy Spirit Night itself
- **Approach:** Alpha-like — presumes genuine spiritual curiosity, not just topical box-checking
- **Framework:** Indwelling (Spirit given at salvation) vs. infilling / baptism of the Spirit (ongoing empowerment/refilling)
- **Goal:** Create an experiential invitation, not just information transfer
