**TLDR:** The official 4-tier volunteer leadership pipeline for Emmanuel Christian Center — Team Member → Leader → Coach → Coordinator. Each level has a definition, responsibilities, span of care, and required training track. This is the content foundation for Nathan Grams' Essential Leadership Track being introduced at TEC 2026.

---

## Overview

- **Authors:** Jodi Ruch and Brandi Hammond
- **Purpose:** Define what leadership advancement means at ECC — both increased responsibility and increased spiritual maturity
- **First public use:** TEC 2026 afternoon location breakouts (see [[tec-planning-meeting-06-01]])

The framework sits on top of **Growth Track** (the entry point for all ECC volunteers). Each level above Team Member requires Growth Track completion plus additional training tracks.

---

## The Four Levels

### 1. Team Member

> A person who is growing in their faith and learning to serve faithfully as part of a ministry team.

- Positions are task-oriented; involve interaction with others but **not leadership of others**
- Focus: developing consistency, reliability, and a heart to serve
- Observed for potential leadership qualities

**Responsibilities:**
- Serve faithfully and reliably
- Engage in personal spiritual growth and community
- Follow direction from leaders
- Complete Growth Track

**Span of Care:** Reports to 1 Leader

---

### 2. Leader

> A person who models spiritual maturity and demonstrates the ability to lead others responsibly.

- Guides Team Members in ministry tasks or small group settings
- Mentors Team Members in spiritual growth and ministry engagement

**Responsibilities:**
- Lead a small group or ministry team
- Mentor and disciple Team Members spiritually and operationally
- Communicate clearly and provide guidance
- Address problems and support volunteers in growth
- Model Christlike character and spiritual disciplines
- Complete Growth Track and Essential Leader Training
- Receive ongoing coaching from a Coach

**Span of Care:** Oversees up to 10 volunteers or group members (i.e. CG leader ratio)

---

### 3. Coach

> A spiritually mature leader who **shepherds other leaders**, providing guidance, encouragement, and accountability.

- Helps Leaders navigate challenges, align with ministry vision, and grow in character and capability

**Responsibilities:**
- Support and develop Leaders spiritually and operationally
- Ensure alignment with ministry vision and culture
- Model spiritual maturity and relational wisdom
- Provide guidance in conflict resolution
- Identify and develop future leaders
- Complete Growth Track, Essential Leader Training, Intermediate Leader Training
- Receive ongoing development from Coordinators

**Span of Care:** Oversees up to 5 Leaders; Reports to 1 Coordinator

---

### 4. Coordinator

> A proven and trusted leader who strengthens leadership across a ministry area.

- Demonstrates spiritual discernment, emotional maturity, and strategic thinking
- Oversees multiple Coaches

**Responsibilities:**
- Oversee ministry systems, schedules, and execution
- Develop Coaches and Leaders spiritually and operationally
- Integrate teams across the ministry and ensure consistent outcomes
- Communicate expectations, priorities, and vision clearly
- Model high standards of spiritual maturity, discernment, and emotional intelligence
- Complete Growth Track, Essential Leader Training, Intermediate Leader Training, Advanced Leader Training
- Receive ongoing development from Directors

**Span of Care:** Oversees up to 5 Coaches; Reports to 1 Director

---

## Training Track Summary

| Level | Required Training |
|-------|------------------|
| Team Member | Growth Track |
| Leader | Growth Track + Essential Leader Training |
| Coach | Growth Track + Essential + Intermediate Leader Training |
| Coordinator | Growth Track + Essential + Intermediate + Advanced Leader Training |

---

## Span of Care Math

A fully staffed Coordinator can theoretically oversee 5 Coaches × 5 Leaders × 10 Team Members = 250 volunteers in a single ministry area. This makes the framework a scalable accountability structure for large volunteer ministries.
