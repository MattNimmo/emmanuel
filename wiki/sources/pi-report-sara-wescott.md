## What the Report Says

**Behavioral style:** Complete extravert — informal and uninhibited; understands people well and uses that to gain friendship and cooperation. Important to her to be liked and accepted. Expresses warmth and enthusiasm to individuals and groups. Easily understands and accepts others' viewpoints; can get diverse groups to collaborate.

**Detail orientation:** Relatively unconcerned about details and inclined to consider them unimportant. Expresses herself in general terms aimed at gaining interest or attention rather than communicating specific, factual information. **Interest in details that are not crucial to success is, at best, casual.** Focuses on big-picture goals.

**Work pace:** Faster than average; can learn quickly but rather generally if left on her own. Because of strong social orientation, **group learning, mentors, and coaches are most effective.**

**Social presence:** Cheerful and upbeat. Makes friends easily. Strongly persuasive; has complete confidence in her ability to gain others' trust and buy-in. Won't take "no" for an answer.

---

## What She Needs to Thrive

- Frequent, regular contact and communication with people
- Recognition for work done right — **rewards that give Sara additional status in the eyes of coworkers are particularly valuable and strong motivators**
- Opportunity to be involved in a variety of different kinds of activities
- Flexible work environment evaluated on results, team-building, or "outside the box" solutions — **not** on following an exact process
- If training on details is necessary, requires intense concentration and discipline; follow-up will be necessary on any specifics

- Socially informal, extroverted, outgoing; gets familiar quickly; communicates in uninhibited, lively, adaptable manner
- Interested in people, building relationships, teamwork rather than technical matters; affable, optimistic, easily trusting
- Focused on goals and the people needed to get there; **not details or plans; frequently delegates details**
- Socially-focused; naturally empathizes with people; positive, non-threatening communication
- Accommodating; most comfortable working with others; often puts team/company goals before personal goals

## Management Implications (Matt managing Sara)

The Promoter profile in the hands of someone managing her means:
- **Do not rely on Sara to self-manage details or follow-through** — she delegates them, forgets them, or considers them unimportant. Kylie (project manager) is the structural answer here.
- **Recognition is currency.** Public acknowledgment in front of peers is more motivating than private or financial recognition.
- **Variety keeps her engaged.** Repetitive, process-heavy sprints without social variety will drain her faster than the work itself.
- **Training on systems must be structured and followed-up** — she cannot learn process by reading a doc; she needs to be walked through it and checked.
- This profile is highly consistent with the Sara entity page note: "Sara is not a 'tenacity' Working Genius — she generates and executes creatively but needs structure to push through the finish line."
