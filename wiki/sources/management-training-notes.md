## Framework: Knowing Your Team

EOS management premise: "We judge ourselves by our motives, we judge others by their actions."

To overcome that gap: "To get others to come to our way of thinking, we must go over to theirs."

Three tools for building team knowledge:

1. **Know their story** — who they are, how they grew up, formative experiences
2. **Personality assessments** — profile who they are; use it to shape how you work with them
3. **Work rhythm assessments**
   - Thinking wavelengths
   - Working Genius
   - The Predictive Index

## Challenges Named (Production Context)

### Headline challenge
"Resetting a team that has been burned out. Culture that has been hurt."

### The core diagnosis
"Natural tenacity in the team — how to train a team that doesn't carry galvanizing [energy] to build a volunteer team focused on vision, not tasks. Which is where we're stuck due to the expectation of excellence and the weight a 'small' mistake carries at broadcast."

Three named challenge areas:
- Expectations
- Vision / leadership
- Development of team

### Sundays specifically
"Stuck managing the tasks due to the amount and expectation — not 'leading' volunteers because of the demands."
