## Nate's Turkey Trip Context

Nate visited all seven sites on a recent trip to Turkey. Key facts shared in the meeting:

- **Laodicea:** Recently excavated (last 5 years; city budget of a Turkish mayor went entirely into excavation). A hidden room behind a corridor was discovered — an early Christian house church, with an ichthys marking the entrance. The church that received the "lukewarm" letter repented within ~50 years; proof is in the persecution-era hidden church. "The worst church in Revelation becomes one of the powerhouses of the church after reading that letter."
- **Ephesus:** Second most excavated city in the world after Cairo. Library of Ephesus is one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
- **Pergamum:** Theater carved into the side of a mountain; 25,000-seat capacity. A martyr's site (early church pastor). "Steeper than Red Rocks."
- **Philadelphia:** Only two stone posts remain in a modern city. Minimal visual material; message will lean on the letter itself.
- **Colossae:** Not yet excavated — just a hill in a valley.
- **Corinth and Athens** also visited (not seven churches but same trip): Mars Hill, Acropolis.
- Guide named Jeremy — gave on-site teachings; recordings captured on Nate's device. Nate offered to share audio via AirDrop to creative team.

## Creative Direction

### Platform / Stage Design

- **Column aesthetic:** Lean into the existing white drapes by crumpling/scrunching them to look like rustic stone columns; uplight to give ancient texture.
- **Gobo projections:** Shoot imagery from Turkey onto the draped columns using gobos.
- **Vertical emphasis:** The tall drape format already makes the platform feel tall and column-like — keep and build on it.
- JC floated: "Almost like a modern ancient collision" — not full archaeology, but not fully contemporary either.

### Lobby / Photo Opportunities

- **Backdrop at each location:** Styrofoam or prop columns + Ephesus Library arch motif + palm trees + greenery.
- **Purpose:** Selfie/photo moment for attendees that anchors them in the series.
- One installation per location; should stay up for the full series run.
- Center screen: rotate weekly to match the specific church's geographic/visual identity.
- Possible B-roll location: Anoka amphitheater (Rum River / Mississippi junction) for rustic ancient-theater feel; JC has biked past it regularly; suggests reaching out to the city of Anoka.

### Promo Video

- **Style:** Narrative voiceover (not travel documentary); ancient footage + modern application; two-minute max.
- **Content mix:** Clips/stills from Turkey trip, excavation footage (Turkey + Anoka as local option), soundbites from Jeremy's on-site teachings, possible additional voiceover.
- **Tone direction:** "This is our history. This still applies to us today." Not sensationalized Revelation; not nostalgia tourism.
- **Reference point:** ECC's prior Joshua series intro — narrative + "join us" structure, ~90 seconds.
- Matt suggested using excerpts from Jeremy's guide teaching audio (Nate has recordings) as voiceover narration alongside B-roll.
- Possible two-narrator approach: historical voice (Jeremy/excavation footage) + pastoral voice (what does this mean for us?).

### Social Media / Week-of Content

- **After-service takeaway interviews:** Short (20 seconds), candid, diverse voices (different ages, backgrounds).
  - Pre-brief 2–3 people to prevent deer-in-headlights; allow some organic captures too.
  - Questions: "What's your takeaway from today?" / "If God wrote you a letter, what would it say?"
  - Post throughout the week.
- **Deeper dives:** Week-of content featuring interviews with the speaker (or trip participants) going deeper on each church's historical context.
- **Excavation angle:** Frame week-of content as "excavating deeper into the message" — ties the archaeological theme to ongoing discipleship.
- Nate cited a church he visited where post-service lobby capture teams ran takeaway interviews at scale. He described it as aspirational — "not expecting us to be there now, but down the road."

### Series Title / Branding

- Primary: **"Seven Churches of Revelation"** (simple, direct)
- Proposed subtitle: **"The Future Is Ancient"** (Matt + JC both responded positively)
- Tour poster concept: list of "cities" (church locations) with dates — like a rock tour schedule; run on lobby screens and in the free-service loop all summer.
