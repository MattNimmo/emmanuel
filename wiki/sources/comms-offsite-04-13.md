## Visionary Projects (What ECC Should Be Doing More Of)

### 180-Style Testimony Videos — Team's #1 Priority

All four independently listed this. Key discussion points:

- **Two-tier format proposed by Matt:**
  - *Quick reflex version* — not phone quality but fast; good mic + camera, turnaround in hours/days
  - *Long mini-doc version* — travel to location, B-roll, deeply produced; do fewer of these
- **Pipeline needed:** Build a shared database/contact list of story leads — anyone who hears a story inputs the person's name, location, and story snapshot; mark follow-up status
- **Jess's idea (from prior church):** Integrate with baptism process — baptism prep interview = built-in testimony capture; 30-second edited clip plays during baptism service
- **Pastor Nate's endorsement:** JC confirmed Nate wants more of these; "as soon as a testimony comes in, it needs to be a reflex"
- **Rebrand consideration:** Possibly rename "180" to something fresh that signals a regular content series

### Mid-Week Devotional / Relatable Social Content (Matt)
- ECC is very Sunday-focused; six other days exist
- Short-format content: Q&A or Reddit-style posts answered by a pastor, shot podcast-style, easily cut for socials
- Not a podcast strategy — just digestible, shareable content for people outside the Sunday rhythm

### Online / YouTube Experience Improvement (Matt)
- Reframe online from "serving regular attenders" to "new guest entry point"
- Improve intentionality; quality improvement benefits both audiences

### Worship Videos & Full Album Execution (Matt)
- The worship recording was fun but stressful (editing timeline, camera/prep gaps)
- Vision: more cameras, more prep time, full marketing strategy — a full year of priority could make it "crazy good"

### Church Environmental Design (Sara)
- See [[church-environmental-design]] for full detail
- ECC has many blank walls; Echo-era artwork looks "Soviet" and should be replaced
- Porch renovation should get a single strong message: "A House of Prayer for All Nations" in white Helvetica
- Instagrammable moments drive organic sharing — people photograph nice spaces

### Online Merch Store (Sara)
- Unified storefront for all Emmanuel merch — existing designs, booklets, devotional packs, stickers, bumper stickers
- Shopify/Letterman integration already proven (used for Dr. Nate Ruch website)
- People constantly asking where to buy sweatshirts, hats, mugs

### Volunteer Development at Locations (Jess)
- Location volunteers (photographers, social media) are hungry for direction
- Even a small investment from central comms staff yields outsized impact
- Idea: build a structured model for comms team to pour into location volunteers

### Relatable / Organic Pastor Content (Matt, end of meeting)
- Content where pastors are in real-life situations, not on a platform
- Less "invisible barrier" between congregation and pastor
- Why-style videos; organic, conversational tone
