## JC's Mid-Service Flow Proposal (Flyover)

JC's proposal for SLP broadcast (details TBD, full discussion deferred):
1. Worship leader does generic "greet your neighbor" (no campus-specific action steps)
2. During that live greeting, scheduled online host greets online guests — happens simultaneously, invisible to in-room attendees
3. Once greeting wraps, hosts come forward for announcements and rest of mid-service

Goal: eliminate the awkward moment where in-room attendees hear the online greeting directed at someone else; make the flow feel more efficient without removing any element.
