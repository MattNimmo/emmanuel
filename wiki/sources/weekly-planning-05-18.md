## Feedback — May 17 Debrief

### What Worked

- **Communication:** Proactive texts and run-through communication made the weekend clear and smooth across locations.
- **Blackout moment:** Pre-warning in the chat (to those who saw it) was appreciated; spared teams a heart attack mid-service.
- **Mallory's last Sunday (campus TBD):** Worship set described as "incredible." Superpower team on platform; Pastor JT present. New worship leader Kylie introduced formally; John (campus pastor) prayed over her. Her first solo week without Dylan was well-received.
- **Maple Grove KB 5-spot:** The Giffords (6-year attendees, heading to mission field) featured — strong local KB moment.
- **Message elements:** Middle school questions intro, letting questions sit unanswered, and testimony were all cited as highly effective. Testimony praised for clarity and communication quality.
- **Central teammates visiting:** Prashant, Jodi, and Brandy visited a location — cited as win for cross-site feedback.
- **Camera shots:** Fewer side shots, more straight-on — improved after prior feedback.

### What Didn't Work / Technical Issues

- **Camera failure:** Zac accidentally pushed camera settings to the live camera instead of the standby camera using the back controller during the illustration segment. Not a hardware issue; won't recur. Screen went black for a few seconds; well-managed pivot.
- **Confidence monitor glitch (SLP):** Countdown monitor possessed/glitching entire morning. Team pivoted by turning a monitor at the sound booth to compensate. Tech appointment already scheduled.
- **Alert communication gap:** Blackout moment was communicated in the Sunday chat only; not everyone saw it. Standard: all technical alerts must go to PC Alive so the full team (including volunteers) sees them, regardless of who is present.

### Missing / Confusing

- **Family overflow context:** Families don't understand the broadcast volume increase or the service culture. Request to add regular explanation to 11am announcements (~once/quarter minimum) and equip high-capacity volunteers and pastors to engage those moments — not just delegate to uncomfortable team members.
