## Meeting Notes — Master Calendar (PDF)

### Announcement Cadence Protocol

Full detail in [[announcement-cadence]]. Summary:
- **4 weeks out:** mention with "save the date" + app call to action; Monday email echoes it
- **2 weeks out:** central announcements begin (full announcement run)
- **Big rocks get 3 announcements; churchwide events get 2** — existing guideline, not new

### App & Website Ownership

- Comms team owns and manages churchwide events in the app
- All items in the orange column of the [[emmanuel-master-calendar]] need an event in the app and website, with a sign-up call to action
- **CCB is locally owned** — each campus manages its own Church Community Builder database

### Protecting Focus

- "Protect the focus of other events" — don't crowd the calendar with competing promotion
- Each event needs a specific date added to the master calendar

### Testimony Pipeline — Action Items

- Need to **build 180s and giving stories into the service flow** — currently ad hoc
- Stages video question: does ECC have a Stages testimony video to use? (open)
- **Kylie assigned:** chase down 6 testimonies for the rest of the year — 3 stewardship, 3 life change
  - These happen on the **last week of each month**
- **Add platform speakers to PCO** (Planning Center Online) — currently not tracked there
