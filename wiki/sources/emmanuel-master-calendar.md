## Recurring Annual Pattern

Based on 2024–2026 analysis, Emmanuel runs a highly consistent annual rhythm:

| Season | Pattern |
|--------|---------|
| January | New year series + KB Catalytic Weekend + Vision Sunday |
| February | Super Sunday (always); new series begins |
| March–April | New series; Good Friday + Easter (full graphics + campaign) |
| April–May | Post-Easter series; Mother's Day; Sisterhood Spring (odd years?) |
| June–August | Summer series (often longest — 9–10 weeks); MNTC Sunday; Father's Day |
| September | Fall Kickoff + CG Campaign Series + booklet |
| October–November | KB-adjacent series; Miracle Offering; Sisterhood Fall |
| December | Christmas advent series (3 weeks) + Christmas Eve |

---

## Key Observations for Capacity Planning

1. **8–9 distinct sermon series per year**, each requiring a full graphics package + bumper video
2. **Hebrews (2024, 10 weeks) and Mark (2025, 9 weeks)** are the longest single series — graphics packages run longer but bumper is one-time
3. **Fall (Sep–Nov) is the most complex period**: CG Campaign + KB series + Miracle Offering + Sisterhood Fall all overlap
4. **December is a hard deadline**: Christmas graphics must be ready before Dec 1 series begins
5. **Super Sunday always in February**: requires promo video, stingers, full package — plan lead time from January
6. **MNTC Sunday (July)** interrupts the summer series every year — content is externally produced, not a comms burden
7. **Vision Sunday and KB Catalytic Weekends always in January** — back-to-back with a new series launch
