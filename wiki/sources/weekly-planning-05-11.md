## Mother's Day Debrief — What Worked

**Flower bouquet moment (MG):** Borrowed from Lakeville's idea — women picked their own flowers from a build-your-own-bouquet setup. "Absolutely a huge win." All ages participated; volunteers helped with bows and bags.

**Photo booths:** People posted using the ECC brand/frame. First time that's generated real social proof. One booth used more than the other, but solid traffic.

**Mother's Day video:** Strong energy; multiple faces across locations; good social build-up leading in. Multiple location pastors praised it.

**One Day to Feed the World + offering:** Celebrating first-time givers integrated into the offering moment; great momentum and energy per Nathan Grams (SLP).

**Prayer over mothers:** Called a "special tradition" by multiple pastors. Felt earned, not forced.

**Worship set:** Strong across the board. Carly praised by Zoe (Lakeville): *"soul and silk"* — two words, said to stick.

**Two piano players at MG:** Javier + Caleb ran two Nords on platform. "Felt awesome." Big vision moment for training up keys players at that campus.

**Service length:** 75 minutes total, including full response song, salvation moment, and women's prayer. That's tight execution.

**Pastor Nate's Mother's Day framing:** Acknowledged the range of experiences people bring into Mother's Day before celebrating the moms in the room. Multiple pastors called it out specifically — "set up the room."

**Nathan (SLP) handled a tech failure:** Charge notes on phone glitched during second service; nobody in the room could tell. Improvised the dedication charges from memory.

## Mother's Day Debrief — What Didn't Work

**Special song (first service):** Gabby's singing partner backed out at 4pm Saturday. She performed solo. Lyrics timing wasn't ready for first service — she was ad-libbing while the team got the words up. Came together; most wouldn't notice. But the miss was upstream: no backup plan for a partner dropout.

**Lobby conversations at SLP:** First impressions team was having a loud conversation *inside the sanctuary* near the end of the message. Aaron Crouse could hear it from FOH. Called out explicitly: getting worse, not better. Action needed on volunteer culture/awareness.

**Camera shots — live production errors:**
- One accidental shot of someone walking up the aisle with what looked like a handheld camera (wasn't)
- One wrong-button switch — brief, but noticeable; brief = only saving grace
- Both trace to volunteer inexperience with live-switch readiness

**Mic left on post-service at MG:** Pastor Jody's mic stayed on after second service; Spanish interpreter heard post-service hallway conversations. Pastor Jody had already told someone not to even wear it. Layered mic kill process — not a simple fix, but needs a protocol.

**Mother's Day video transition timing:** The bumper countdown in PCO showed ~30 seconds when it was actually ~10. Caused miscue on when Pastor Jody should walk to stage. Combination of first time with the video + wrong countdown + no prior watchthrough. Fix: run through the video in full during tech rehearsal so everyone knows the cue.

**Child dedication timing:** 14 families at SLP → parents managing active kids through 17–18 minutes of worship created visible stress. One family recommended moving dedication earlier (between songs 2–3, simultaneous with worship playing, like communion). Greed from multiple pastors. ELK had zero families; SLP had one signup total.

**Event/staff weight — JC's flag:** The Thursday before had Pulse event + NCU Commencement rehearsal/strike + production reset for National Day of Prayer. Friday had commencement. Sunday had child dedication + Mother's Day. Josh lived outside the tent. JC called it calendar planning: *"messaging can get cloudy, staff gets heavy."* Not a criticism of any one event — a flag to be more intentional about stacking external events near high-demand Sundays.
