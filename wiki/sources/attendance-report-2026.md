## Year-Over-Year Context (2025 Comparison Column)

The report includes last-year totals for most weeks. Notable comparisons:

| Event | 2026 | 2025 | Change |
|-------|------|------|--------|
| Easter | 6,993 | 3,895 | **+79%** |
| Super Sunday | 4,830 | 5,162 | -6% |
| Mother's Day | 4,294 | 4,267 | +0.6% |
| Palm Sunday (Mar 29) | 4,086 | 6,200 | -34% (Easter timing diff) |
| Fall Kickoff (Sep 13) | — | 3,787 | — |
| Miracle Offering (Nov 8) | — | 4,084 | — |

**Note on Palm Sunday 2025:** The 6,200 figure appears to be Easter 2025 attendance misaligned — Easter 2025 falls in April, but the comparison column shows 6,200 for Mar 29 2025. Treat with caution; likely a column offset artifact.

**Easter 2026 growth is the standout signal.** Nearly double Easter 2025 — significant. May reflect a changed date, aggressive outreach, or organic growth. Worth investigating what drove the increase before attributing it to any single factor.
