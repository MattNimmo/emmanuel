## Wrong Video Incident — Full Debrief

### Pastor Darren's version (more complete than creative team's)
- Brad sent **complete clarity** to Matt and Darren on Tuesday: service order with notes + exactly **one video** intended for the message.
- The other videos in the system were *promo content* from the ODTFTW lead-up weeks — not from Brad.
- Darren had Matt and Brad communicating directly so he wouldn't be a middleman.
- His concern: if Josh and Matt were both present at Thursday run-through and that video played, **nobody watched the content critically enough** to notice it was a promo-to-organizations video, not a congregation video.

### Team response
- Matt acknowledges: "That's our miss for sure." The Thursday check happened, but the wrong video was in place and nobody caught that the content was mismatched for the context.
- JC: Content check needs to shift to be more of a service flow run-through. Checklist-driven. Team needs to actually *watch* video content, not just verify it loaded.
- **Fix agreed:** Guest speaker content goes to `message` group email, not just Matt's inbox. All team eyes on one package.

### Josh's run-through command presence
- Darren noted Josh didn't feel "totally in command of the moment" during live stream check — contrast with when Matt leads (which felt clear to LPs and speakers).

## Guest Speaker Protocol — Call Time

- Brad was set for 7:45am; Nate moved it to 8:00am for hotel logistics. Matt notified morning-of.
- 8am worked for this situation. Darren made the broader point: guest speakers vary significantly. Some (like Susie Larson) are "I'm already ready, just be ready for me." Others (like Rob Hoskins at Miracle Offering) may have full riders.
- Darren's concern: if a *production director* sets a call time that feels early to a high-profile speaker, it creates an awkward first impression, even if the speaker is humble.
- **Action:** JC committed to developing a guest speaker checklist / written protocol. Will be owned by production.
