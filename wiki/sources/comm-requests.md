**TLDR:** A year-in-review list of recurring communications requests organized by month, compiled by Pastor Jodi. Covers ~34 annual events/campaigns with columns for graphics, video, social, website/app, and other needs. Does not include time estimates.

---

## Overview

- **Author:** Pastor Jodi Ruch
- **Type:** Annual communications request map
- **Format:** Excel, single sheet (50 rows × 10 columns)
- **Purpose:** Establishes what one year of communications output looks like from a ministry calendar perspective

## Key Observations

- **34 distinct events/campaigns** identified in a single year
- Many rows have sparse deliverable data — likely reflects what's been asked for historically, not a complete scope
- No time estimates exist — ⚠️ **TO DO: gather time estimates per deliverable type** (see [[comms-team-deliverables]])
- Deliverables vary significantly in scope — Super Sunday and Easter are multi-channel; some entries (Avalanche, ELC Graduation) have no listed deliverables
- The doc focuses on *what is requested*, not *what capacity it requires*
