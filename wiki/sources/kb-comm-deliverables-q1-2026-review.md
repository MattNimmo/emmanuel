## Key Observations

- **Miracle Offering is significantly larger than the [[comm-requests]] entry suggests.** The Comm Requests doc lists only "Engagement card" for KB Catalytic Weekend #2 in November. This document reveals the full scope: 12 deliverables spanning video, print, digital, data graphics, email, and app/web.
- **The 4 data slides** (per-location project/goal, Emmanuel goal, year-to-date) are **post-event deliverables** — KB provides giving results ~1 week after Miracle Offering Sunday. These land ~Nov 15, not before the event. No pre-event data wait.
- **The giving envelope is a print deliverable** — requires extra lead time for printing and distribution.
- **Two emails** (All Church + Follow-up) are comms team output, not just pastoral communications.

---

## Implications for Capacity Planning

- November KB + Miracle Offering deliverables should be treated as a **full major event package** (~equal to Easter or Christmas in scope)
- 8 deliverables are pre-event; 4 data slides are post-event (~Nov 15) after KB provides giving results — spreads the load but extends the window into mid-November
- Print items (giving envelopes) require ordering lead time — typically 2–3 weeks before offering Sunday
