## What This Document Is

A single shared .docx updated after every Weekend Planning Meeting. Entries appear in reverse chronological order (newest at top). Each entry contains:

1. **Meeting date**
2. **Upcoming 2-week service plans** — hosting, handouts, special elements, central + local announcements per campus
3. **Debrief of the prior Sunday** — the 4 helpful questions format

This is the team's own notes, distinct from AI-generated transcripts. It's briefer and structured differently — bullet-point wins and misses attributed to each person by first name or "P[Name]".

The document ends (chronologically at the start) with Slack submissions from JC, Bryan, Matt, Stefan, and James proposing how the Monday morning meeting should be structured. ## Standards Established (April 2025 Sessions)

Two back-to-back meetings (Apr 6 and Apr 14, 2025) were spent establishing Sunday execution standards. Pastor Nate drove this directly.

**Sound:**
- Standard: congregation should be able to hear other people singing; it's church, not a show
- LPs have final word on sound day-of
- Training needed: younger board operators don't hear the room the way attendees do
- Applies to youth services as well
- Over 10 families had left ECC in 12 months over volume

**Lighting:**
- "Sunrise/sunset, not lightning bolts"
- Bright enough to read a physical Bible at the last row
- Dark room doesn't build trust with new guests; target demographic (young families) want less sensory stimulation
- No excessive movement-lighting during message

**Haze:**
- Definitive decision: "I'm done." (Pastor Nate, Apr 14)
- In-room experience takes priority over video aesthetics

**Dress code / Platform appearance:**
- Everything should feel unified; paid staff sets the top standard for Team Emmanuel
- Lookbook idea raised (not yet created)
- Hats: generally no, exceptions possible (JC cited as example)
- Platform = highest visibility; character in other spaces matters too
- LPs lead their teams on this

- PCO timers not working at locations — appears 10+ times across the doc
- Countdown not firing, PCO Live dropping connections at location confidence monitors

**Camera shots:**
- Shaky rover/handheld shots flagged repeatedly (Mar 2026 through present)
- Side shots creating background inconsistency — raised multiple times before the May 2026 director sync formalized the feedback
- Speaker not knowing which camera is live — flagged Apr 2026; tally light fix on the list

**Lobby TVs:**
- Not working at MG, ELK, or LV multiple times (separate incidents)
- Who owns lobby TV management is an unresolved tension — appears in Jun 2025, Jan 2026, and May 2026

**Late content / notes:**
- Guest speaker notes arriving late is the single most common "didn't work" entry
- Comms and production can't prepare screen content without early notes
- Standard expectation for internal speakers: notes by Thursday before Sunday recording

**Translation/Spanish interpretation:**
- Resi issues affect the translation feed
- Name/language consistency ("campuses" vs. location language)
- LV and other locations have Spanish speakers who need the feed to work

**Child dedication form:**
- Centrally managed form causing confusion and missed sign-up windows — flagged in Feb 2025, Mar 2025, and the Mother's Day 2026 failure
- Resolution: moving to location-level management (Nathan Grams / Apr–May 2026)

## Recurring What-Worked Themes

**What consistently lands:**
- Strong altar responses / open worship response moments
- Baptism Sunday — energy, cameras, and crowd response consistently strong
- Nathan Grams' offering moments at SLP — praised multiple times for being personal and compelling
- Guest speakers who do their homework on prior sermons and series
- Choir Sundays — "always a win" (Nathan Grams)
- Personalized local elements (shoutouts to coaches, local leaders, specific stories)
- JC flexing into worship or service unexpectedly
- Having central staff attend locations (Matt at ELK, Audrey at MG, PNate visiting)
- Tight, focused announcements — "one announcement" weeks praised; bulletin-board weeks flagged

**What doesn't:**
- Multiple voices/elements in a single service (too many announcements, too many special elements, too many leaders on platform)
- Long services — consistently flagged; 75-80 minutes is the sweet spot
- New songs without warm-up context; "unfamiliar" worship sets harder to engage
- Bumper-to-message transitions without proper lighting cue
- Any tech issue that breaks the Resi feed mid-message at locations
