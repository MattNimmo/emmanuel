**TLDR:** Matt's sales call with Caroline (ClickUp) confirmed ClickUp as the chosen platform — pricing is the last gate. Small Business Suite for 10 users quoted at $6,375/yr on a 2-year contract (25% off list). Onboarding via ClickUp Assist is included and planned.

---

## Context

- Matt Nimmo evaluating ClickUp for the ECC comms/creative team
- Previous tools: Monday (most recent), Asana — both used without a dedicated PM
- Kylie (new PM) starts in two weeks; ClickUp setup needs to be ready
- Team size: comms = 5 people; full creative (production + worship + comms) = 10–12

---

## Why ClickUp

Primary driver: **capacity visibility**. Matt's first question when he stepped into the role was "how do we know where we're at four weeks from now?" Monday didn't have a comparable Workload view. Key needs:

- **Workload/capacity view** — see per-person utilization by week
- **Calendar + capacity integration** — plan 12 months ahead for recurring events
- **AI automations** — e.g., notify PM if an intake request goes 48 hours without response
- **Consolidation** — replace Monday, Asana, and the Claude-based local knowledge base with a single platform

## Pricing

| Package | Users | List Price | Discount | Annual Cost |
|---------|-------|-----------|----------|------------|
| Small Business Suite | 10 | $8,500 | 25% (2-yr contract) | **$6,375/yr** |
| Small Business Suite | 5 | ~$4,250 | Similar discount | ~$3,187/yr |
| Small Business Suite | 20 | $16,000 | TBD | TBD |

- Packages go in steps: 5, 10, 20, 30 — no 15-user option
- Annual only — no month-to-month
- Discounts on multi-year contracts transfer when upgrading (prorated at same % off)
- Matt requested 5/10/20-user pricing at 12 and 24 months for internal approval

### AI Credit System
- 50,000 credits/month for 10-user package
- Overage: +100k credits/month for $100 (amendment to contract)
- Standard usage (asking questions, querying docs) does not consume credits; image generation and super agents do
- HIPAA-compliant AI; data not stored/trained on

---

## What's Included in Small Business Suite

- Enterprise ClickUp (no feature limits)
- ClickUp Brain — multi-LLM (Claude, ChatGPT available via Brain)
- ClickUp Assist — 24 hours of 1:1 onboarding support (dedicated resource)
  - First 12 hours: once/week for 3 months to get live
  - Remaining 12 hours: banked for questions, new workflows, team training
- Premium support: 1-hour email SLA, dedicated rep
- Docs/wiki/SOPs with AI query layer
