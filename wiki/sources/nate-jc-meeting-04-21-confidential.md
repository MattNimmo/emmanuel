## Comms Priority Framework — Matt's Presentation

Matt walked Nate through the capacity model and priority tiers:

**Capacity baseline:**
- ~26–29 hrs effective per person
- Sunday baseline (YouTube clips, podcast upload, platforms, socials, thumbnails, emails) = 7–10 hrs/person on Mondays/Tuesdays
- Leaves ~19 hrs/person available for project work

**Priority tiers presented:**
| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Recurring baseline** | Keeps the church running every week — non-negotiable |
| **High impact / growth-driving / Sunday-related** | Series, invitational events (Easter, Christmas, Super Sunday, Fall Kickoff), campaign content; always yes; never outsourced |
| **Member-serving / internal** | Connect group materials, department support; yes as capacity allows |
| **Volunteer/dept. self-serve** | Youth party graphics, kids event content — dept. owns it; comms provides resources via Comms Resource Channel |

**180 videos:** Matt placed these in the **high-impact / growth-driving** tier — not capacity-allows. Nate agreed.

**Outsourcing:** Connect group series videos are candidates for outsourcing. Nate noted he tried to outsource his book series videos; Audrey refused and wanted to own all marketing. Budget trail is unclear — Jodi used to have outsource budget for Connect production; unknown if that carried over to Audrey's plan. JC and Matt to work through with Jimmy.

**Nate's standing rule on outsourcing:** If we sunset or hand something off, communicate it publicly — don't just quietly drop it internally.
