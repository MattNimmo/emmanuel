## Decision 2 — August Series: "The Storyteller" (RESOLVED)

The August series — previously listed as TBD in [[message-planning-meeting-05-04]] — is now decided.

**Title:** *The Storyteller*
**Run:** August 9 – September 6, 2026 (five weeks)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Theme | Stories Jesus told; all gospels, not just Luke or Matthew |
| Title rationale | "Parables" felt less accessible to guests; "The Storyteller" chosen |
