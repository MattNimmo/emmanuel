## Implications for Comms Planning

This document is the **earliest forward-look** the wiki has for 2027 production planning. Cross-reference against [[comms-project-horizon-2026-2027]] and [[comms-weekly-capacity-hours-2026-2027]] to identify:

1. **Q1 2027 load** — Vision Sunday (1/17), Super Sunday (2/14), Avalanche (2/19–20), Water Baptism (2/28) put four major churchwide moments into a 6-week window. Same compression as 2026.
2. **Easter window** — Good Friday (3/26) → Easter (3/28) — same back-to-back structure as 2026. Plan for it.
3. **Fall stack** — Sisterhood Night (11/5) **immediately** before Miracle Offering (11/7) is a tighter cadence than 2026 (Miracle Offering was Nov 9 in 2026). This is a real production risk window for the comms team.
4. **MNTC + Water Baptism + Youth Summer Camp** stack — Jul 11, 18, and 19–23 — three major moments in 12 days. Plan capacity early.
