### Mar 2025 – Nov 2025: Highlights and Notable Observations

> Full element-by-element data for this period requires consultation of the raw PDF files (March 9, 2025 – November 30, 2025). The observations below capture confirmed patterns and specific exceptions from the initial reading pass.

#### Host Pastor / New Guest
- **Standard plan:** 1:00
- **Typical actuals (9am / 11am):** 1:05–1:30 range; consistently over plan by at least 5 seconds, often 20–30 seconds
- **Elevated instances:**
  - Sep 14, 2025 (Fall Kickoff): 2:05 / 2:15 — nearly double plan
  - Special Sundays (Easter, MNTC, Water Baptism): regularly 1:30–1:45
- **Pattern:** 1:00 plan functions as a floor, not a target. Near-weekly overrun.

#### Greet and Seat
- **Standard plan:** 0:20 (most weeks); 0:45 on weeks with online host integration or chair changeovers
- **Typical actuals:** Generally within 10–15 seconds of plan; spikes to 0:45+ when "Online Host" is added or room configuration changes (chairs out/in)
- **No significant outliers confirmed** in this period

#### Offering
- **Standard plan:** 2:10
- **Typical actuals (9am / 11am):** 2:00–2:45 range; 9am slightly tighter, 11am tends higher
- **Specific elevated instances:**
  - Jun 1, 2025: plan 3:10, actual 2:09 — underrun (unusual; plan was elevated for special push)
  - Jul 6, 2025: 3:52 / 3:41 (plan 2:10) — significantly over; cause unknown
  - Nov 2, 2025: **4:28 / 4:01** (plan 2:10) — Miracle Offering Sunday; offering was campaign moment
  - Nov 23, 2025: **3:50 / 4:18** (plan 3:30) — MO update week; elevated plan still underestimated

#### Announcements
- **Standard plan:** 1:20–1:30; elevated to 2:00–2:30 during campaign-heavy weeks
- **Typical overrun:** 15–60 seconds in standard weeks; 60–90 seconds over when campaign content (KB, Miracle Offering, seasonal events) is added
- **Dual-block weeks:** When both a primary and secondary announcement block appear, combined time regularly reaches 2:30–3:00+
- **Timer error:** Sep 14, 2025 11am announcements = 4528:29 — column excluded (see Data Quality)

## Key Patterns Across Full Dataset

1. **11am always runs longer than 9am.** Typical gap: 5–10 minutes. Gap widens on high-energy or extended-response Sundays (Vision Sunday: 9.5 min gap; MLK Sunday: only 0:23 gap because both were far over).

2. **Plan is consistently optimistic.** Virtually no standard week comes in at or under plan for both services. The 9am is occasionally within range; 11am almost never is.

3. **Message is the primary overrun driver.** PNate regularly runs 5–15 minutes over planned message length. Worst case in confirmed data: Jan 18, 2026 (+17–18 min) due to embedded MLK Moment + KB Video. Guest preachers more variable.

4. **Announcements compound overrun.** Standard weeks: +15–30 sec. Campaign weeks: +60–90 sec. Super Sunday / high-content weeks: up to +2:00 over plan. Content added last-minute (KB engagement cards, event promos) appears to be primary cause.

5. **Offering rarely the culprit — except campaign weeks.** Standard weeks land 2:00–2:45 for a 2:10 plan. Miracle Offering weeks are a reliable exception (+1:30 to +2:00 over plan).

6. **Host Pastor reliably over.** The 1:00 plan functions as a floor. Near-weekly overrun of 5–45 seconds; elevated to 1:30–2:15 on special Sundays.

7. **KB 5 Spot has never come in at plan.** Every confirmed appearance runs over 5:00. Range: +46 seconds (Sep 7) to +5:18 (Oct 26 KB Launch).

8. **11am Greet and Seat frequently spills.** Planned 0:20–0:45; 11am frequently hits 0:45–1:30 when online host moment is added or room layout changes mid-service.
