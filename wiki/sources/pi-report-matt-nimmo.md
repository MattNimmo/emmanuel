## What the Report Says

**Behavioral style:** Helpful, patient, and stable. Works steadily and consistently. Respectful of established processes — prefers having and following a well thought-out process. If responsible for establishing the process, does so in a thoughtful, methodical manner with close attention to detail. Once established, expects it to be followed consistently.

**With people:** Pleasant, agreeable, supportive. Derives satisfaction from helping others. Likes to feel part of a secure team. Unassuming, friendly in general contact. Patient, focused listener — especially with people he knows well. Sympathetic; wants to help whenever possible.

**Decision-making:** Careful. Takes time to follow the established process, examine different angles, and explore enough to ensure few surprises. Even-tempered in how he communicates and coaches.

**Change response:** Takes time to learn new responsibilities thoroughly before acting. Gets the "lay of the land" first. This is true whether initiating changes (done only after careful thought and research) or responding to changes being asked of him.

**One notable nuance:** PI says "casual with rules; focuses on goal not how to get there." This seems to run counter to the BigFive C=91 profile. The PI description is likely describing delegation behavior — Matt is goal-oriented and won't over-police the path his team takes — rather than a lack of personal standards. The high Conscientiousness is about his *own* work quality; the "casual with rules" applies to what he asks of others.
