## Debrief: May 4 — Brad Rosenberg (ODTFTW Guest Speaker)

### What Worked
- **Cameras:** Location pastor Jeff gave strongest camera feedback yet; "most cohesive" all-location look, less stark contrast between angles
- **Brad's message:** Kept people engaged, humor, emotional moment (kid + semi truck story), strong giving action step
- **Service timing:** Message length felt perfect; set up the offering moment well
- **Nathan/Analia hosting:** Nate specifically praised; smooth transition to online audience; felt natural and developed
- **JT in worship:** Stepped in well; Mallory was on vacation (ending her role at ECC)
- **Zac's camera positioning:** Caught Brad's giving illustration from perfect angle in second service without any rehearsal
- **Salvations at SLP:** "A ton of people raised their hands" — full services
- **Clean message-to-offering handoff:** Sara praised; salvation call placed appropriately between message and offering (not lost in the larger moment)
