## Not (What Didn't Work)

### Wrong video in service
- A wrong ODTFTW promo video was loaded in place of Brad's message-intended video.
- **Root cause:** Five different videos existed for this week. Without explicit labeling of which video was Brad's "for-message" video, the team made a best guess at Thursday content check and guessed wrong.
- **Accountability:** JC and Matt own it as leads; Josh loaded what the link gave him and didn't independently question it (though he noted it "seemed like it was from 2010" — worth catching).
- **Fix decided:** All guest speaker content should route through the `message` group email (not just to Matt) so all eyes are on the same package. Thursday content check needs a formal checklist.

### Nate's mic — second consecutive week
- Aaron wasn't on his phone when Josh texted the mic-switch alert. A hand-off in the moment recovered it, but the pattern is worth addressing.

### Guest speaker call time confusion
- Brad was originally set for 7:45am arrival. Nate shifted it to 8:00am (hotel checkout logistics). Matt was notified morning-of. Caused Josh to be "ready for something different."
- 8:00am worked, but the process for negotiating call time was reactive.

### Offering graphic/screen content ambiguity
- Three different graphics existed for the offering moment; no one specified which was for 9am vs. 11am.
- Zac went improvised; Lakeville production only knew the right sequence because Pastor Darren was physically present and directed them.
- Fix: Specific ordering of offering content should be documented in PCO or the service flow doc by Thursday.
