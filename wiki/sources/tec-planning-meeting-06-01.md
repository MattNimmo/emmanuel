## New Element: Essential Leadership Track

Nathan Grams is introducing the **Essential Leadership Track** — a structured 4-level leadership pipeline being launched as the content backbone for afternoon location breakouts. The track is a follow-up to Growth Track and defines four levels: **Team Member → Leader → Coach → Coordinator** (see [[leadership-levels-playbook]] for the full framework; concept page at [[leadership-pipeline]]).

**Format being explored for breakouts:**
- Central breakouts: 30–45 min vision-level teaching (casting the why), then internal breakout into subtopics using a "4 corners" format
- Location breakouts: use the leadership level framework as the anchor; location pastor facilitates
- Session contributors: identified high-capacity volunteers and staff invited to teach 15-min segments within sessions (e.g. Ryan on electric guitar for worship team tech track)
- Key tension: sessions must serve both first-year volunteers and 20-year veterans; don't default to basics for the whole group

**Nathan's framing (direct quote):** "We're working on building the essential leadership track, which is a follow up to Growth Track. Years ago, we introduced Growth Track at Team Emmanuel Conference. This time, we could incorporate some of the leadership teachings in the general sessions and then use the afternoon location breakout as a timed vision cast for the different roles and levels of leadership."

## Logistics Lessons from 2025

**Lunch (Chick-fil-A):**
- Requires **2-week pre-order deadline** (hard constraint from vendor)
- Distribute across upstairs and downstairs; 60/40 split (not 50/50) toward higher-traffic floor
- Communicate lunch locations on floor map in booklet and on lobby screens
- Label gluten-free/vegetarian station clearly and separately
- 2025 failure: dietary accommodations were delivered incorrectly; had to scramble on-site
- Don't penalize walk-ins for food access, but hold early registrants as priority (no hard voucher system — too complex to manage)
- Chick-fil-A childcare cost for staff: ECC pays (confirmed for registered staff/part-time)

**Registration:**
- Early-registration prizes / drawings improved 2025 numbers; keep
- Communication to staff: DLT (not the planning team) owns internal staff communication about hours, assignments, expectations

**Signage:**
- Directional signs on stanchions (printed last minute in 2025 — build them earlier)
- Lobby screens should show room assignments and directional info day-of
- 2025 gap: people didn't know lunch was available upstairs

**Booklet (carried from 2025):**
- Schedule; morning breakout sessions (ministry-specific); floor map (floor 1, floor 2); afternoon breakout sessions (location-specific); notes pages per session; top 3 takeaways page; QR code for Emmanuel app
- Include session descriptions in advance so attendees can pre-plan — strong request from this meeting
