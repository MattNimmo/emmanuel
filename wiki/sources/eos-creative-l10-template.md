## What This Is

A template Matt drafted to restructure the Creative Team's Monday weekly meeting using EOS Level 10 format. It sits at the bottom of a three-meeting Monday cascade:

| Time | Meeting | Who | Purpose |
|------|---------|-----|---------|
| 9:30am | Service Planning | Creative Team + Pastoral Staff | Next 2 weekends + horizon scan |
| Late morning | Directors Sync | Location Pastors + Matt & JC | Align direction; set creative team marching orders |
| Afternoon | **Creative Team L10** | Production + Comms + Worship | Execute. Solve issues. Commit to to-dos. |

By the time the L10 starts, Matt and JC should have: (1) decisions from Directors Sync, (2) changes from the 9:30am plan, (3) cascading messages to deliver. These shape the Headlines and Issues sections.

---

## The 90-Minute Agenda

| Time | Section | What Happens |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 5 min | Segue | Each person: one personal best + one ministry best. No ministry problems yet — this resets the room. |
| 5 min | Scorecard Review | 5–10 weekly metrics. Each owner: "on track" or "off track." Anything off drops to Issues. |
| 5 min | Rock Review | Quarterly priorities. Each owner: "on track" or "off track," one sentence max. Off-track → Issues. |
| 5 min | Headlines | One-sentence updates: wins, vendor changes, congregant feedback, equipment. Cascading messages from Directors Sync. Anything needing a decision → Issues. |
| 5 min | To-Do Review | Walk last week's to-dos. Each item: done or not done. Target 90% completion. |
| 60 min | IDS | Identify, Discuss, Solve. Rank issues 1–5 by priority. Solve top issues until time's up. |
| 5 min | Conclude | Recap to-dos (owners verbally agree). Cascading messages. Rate meeting 1–10; anything under 8 gets a sentence of why. |

## Proposed Scorecard (Starter — Creative Team)

| Metric | Owner | Weekly Target |
|--------|-------|--------------|
| Service elements locked by Wednesday EOD (next Sunday) | Production Lead | 100% |
| Worship setlist + chord charts published to team | Worship Lead | By Tuesday EOD |
| Sermon graphics / motion delivered | Comms Lead | By Thursday EOD |
| Social posts scheduled (sermon series + announcements) | Comms Lead | Week ahead, by Friday |
| ProPresenter slides built & QA'd | Production Lead | By Saturday noon |
| Volunteer slots filled (audio, video, lyrics, camera) | Production Lead | 100% by Friday |
| Post-service debrief notes logged | Rotating | By Monday 9am |
| Open creative requests (intake → assigned) | Comms Lead | ≤5 in queue |

**EOS guidance on scorecards:** 5–15 metrics is the sweet spot. Every metric needs a single owner and a binary target (hit or miss). Prefer leading indicators (was the work done on time?) over lagging ones (Sunday attendance).

## Rock Examples for Creative Team

Rocks = 90-day priorities. 3–7 per quarter. One owner each.

- Launch redesigned ECC website by end of Q2
- Build sustainable volunteer pipeline for camera operators (target: 8 trained)
- Standardize sermon series visual rollout (one repeatable kit: motion, social, print, ProPresenter)

---

## Non-Negotiables (From EOS)

- Same day, same time, every week. Moving it signals it's not a priority.
- Same agenda, same order, every week.
- Time-boxed. Start on time, end on time.
- Drop, don't debate. When something needs discussion during Scorecard/Rocks/Headlines — add to Issues, don't talk it out now.
- Solve, don't just discuss. Every IDS issue ends with a solution, a to-do, or a deliberate "table until X."

---

## What Makes a Good To-Do

Specific, actionable, finishable in 7 days, one owner.

| Bad | Good |
|-----|------|
| "Improve our social media" | "Draft 5 Instagram captions for the new sermon series and send to Matt by Thursday" |
| "Fix the volunteer situation" | "Book 2 camera volunteers for May 17 by Friday — Jess owns" |
