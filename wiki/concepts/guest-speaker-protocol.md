**TLDR:** A formal guest speaker protocol doesn't exist yet — the May 4 wrong-video incident and call-time confusion triggered a commitment to build one. Key decisions already made; full checklist to be drafted by production.

## Why This Matters

Guest speaker Sundays introduce more unknowns than regular weeks: different message content, unfamiliar video packages, varied speaker preferences, call-time logistics. The May 4 incident (wrong video played in service) and call-time confusion during Brad Rosenberg's visit exposed that the team was improvising these moments rather than running a defined process.

JC: "We won't waver from [the protocol]. If we need to flex for a speaker, we'll talk about it."

---

## What's Decided

### Content routing
All guest speaker content (notes, videos, service order) must be sent to the **`message` group email** — not just to Matt's inbox. This ensures all production team eyes are on the same package before Thursday content check.

### Thursday content check — evolved
The Thursday session must treat guest-speaker content as the *only* video package. With ODTFTW weeks, multiple promo videos existed in the system; the team guessed wrong which one was the "message video." Specific labeling and sequential review of content in service order is required.

### Video content review
If a video plays during the message, **someone must watch it** before Thursday is called done — not just verify it loaded. The wrong video was a clearly-out-of-context organization-pitch video; critical viewing would have caught it immediately.

### Call time
- Standard ask: **7:45am** (JC default)
- If speaker requests later: Matt + JC decide, communicate to team morning-of at minimum
- Target: build a written run-through document so any call time change doesn't create gaps in what the speaker experiences
- High-profile speakers (e.g., Rob Hoskins for Miracle Offering) may require custom arrangements — treat like a rider conversation, not a default

### What to give the speaker at arrival
JC: "Here is what we need from you. Communicate anything you need from us at this time. Here is what we need from you. Great — we're done with those three things." Build a 1-page arrival checklist for guest speakers covering:
- Mic check process
- Video/content confirmation ("Is this the video you intended?")
- What the live stream check involves and why
- What the run-through is and what you need from them
