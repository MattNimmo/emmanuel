**TLDR:** Emmanuel Worship is ECC's multi-campus worship team led by Pastor JonCarlos Velez. It runs across all four physical campuses plus online, operates off Planning Center and Ableton, and holds members to clear role-specific expectations and a defined code of ethics.

## Mission & Identity

Emmanuel's mission — "For everyone to know Jesus, grow together and live with purpose" — is what the worship team exists to facilitate. Their role: provide an expressive, innovative, and engaging worship experience that encourages everyone to go after God and connect with Him on a deeper level.

### Three Identity Values
1. **Postured for Praise** — public worship is an overflow of private time with Jesus
2. **Humble** — "We are built off the sacrifices of many, not the talents of a few"
3. **Prepared** — play skillfully and with a joyful sound (Psalm 33:3)

## Team Structure & Roles

| Role | Function |
|------|----------|
| Worship Leader (WL) | Sets spiritual and musical direction for the service; all other roles follow the WL |
| MD (Music Director) | Bridge between WL, band, and production; owns Ableton, song form communication, transitions |
| FLVs (Front Line Vocalists) | "Thermostat, not a thermometer" — lead with energy and expression; support the WL |
| Piano | Lead on chord changes and flow moments; tonal rhythm |
| Keys | Sonic energy; lead transitions; always available to support speaker moments |
| Acoustic Guitar | Percussive co-leader with piano and drums; tonal rhythm + structure in down moments |
| Electric Guitar | Lead lines + chord voicings; fill space; swell with dynamic |
| Bass | Sonic foundation; lock with drummer for pocket; drive big moments with 8th notes |
| Drums | Dynamic energy leader; rhythmic backbone; follow WL and MD |

### MD Technical Requirements
The MD must know **Ableton**: launch tracks and change clicks, cut tracks mid-song. The MD is the production liaison — communicates service flow changes directly to the service producer.

## Operational Systems

- **Planning Center** — set lists, notes, and rehearsal materials posted a few days before each service
- **Ableton** — click tracks and playback; MD-operated
- **Hand signals** — standardized system for real-time song direction (see [[worship-handbook-v6]] for full chart)
- **Nashville Number System** — required knowledge for MDs

## Platform Standards

### Preparation
- Materials come from Planning Center; members prepare independently before rehearsal
- Music is memorized — no music stands or sheet music on platform
- Prepare privately; public worship is the overflow of private devotional time

### Rehearsal Culture
- Punctual and ready at all scheduled times
- Prepared to make changes mid-rehearsal as the team leans into what the service needs
- Excellence standard: soundcheck and rehearsal are not the time to learn your part

## Platform Attire

See [[worship-lookbook-v2]] for the full guide. Summary:
- Earth tones, layers, solids — pastels and cool colors work best on broadcast cameras
- No white (glows), no tight patterns (moiré), no words/logos on shirts, no shorts on platform
- Fit rules: shirt must cover stomach (arms raised), shoulders, back (kneeling)
- Broadcast: skin tone matters — pale = avoid black; darker skin = avoid white
- Always bring a backup outfit

## Team Ethics

### Platform Accountability
Being on platform means a higher level of public accountability. You represent the church to members and outside parties at all times.

### Biblical Sexuality
Emmanuel holds the orthodox view: marriage is between one man and one woman. Leaders maintain a higher standard in all relationships — no pornography or premarital sexual activity (physical or digital).

### Social Media
Online presence reflects the church even when ECC isn't mentioned. Think before posting. "We have a responsibility to be a testimony in the culture."

### Alcohol
- No alcohol on church property, at church events, or at Emmanuel-sponsored gatherings
- No alcohol in the company of minors you lead
- Thoughtful discretion elsewhere as a reflection on Christ and the church

### Substance Abuse
Any involvement with drugs or alcohol that damages the church or ministry reputation is unacceptable. Location worship staff are a resource, not just a reporting mechanism.

### Conflict & Care
No gossip. Conflicts addressed directly. Location Worship Staff provide pastoral support for personal crises — reach out by email, text, or in person.
