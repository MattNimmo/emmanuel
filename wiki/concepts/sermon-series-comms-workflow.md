**TLDR:** Every new sermon series at Emmanuel triggers a standard set of comms deliverables. This workflow repeats 8–9 times per year and is the most predictable, recurring unit of comms workload. It is the foundation of the ClickUp capacity template.

---

## Triggers

A new sermon series workflow begins when:
- A new series title appears on the [[emmanuel-master-calendar]]
- Pastor Nate / leadership confirms the series name and direction
- Series is far enough out that design lead time is achievable

Emmanuel runs **8–9 distinct sermon series per year** (based on 2024–2025 data).

## Announcement Cadence

Every series (and churchwide event) follows a defined promotion cadence. This governs when deliverables are deployed, not just when they're produced. Source: [[meeting-with-nathan-master-calendar]].

| Timing | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **4 weeks before series launch** | "Save the date" mention in service; event live in app with sign-up CTA; Monday email echoes with the 4-week callout |
| **2 weeks before launch** | Central announcements begin — full dedicated announcement slot(s) run through launch weekend |
| **Monday email (ongoing)** | Echoes the churchwide at each cadence stage throughout the series |

### Announcement Slot Count

| Event Type | Slots |
|------------|-------|
| **Big rocks** (KB, Miracle Offering, Easter, Christmas, etc.) | **3** |
| **Churchwide events** | **2** |

A "slot" = a full dedicated announcement. A "mention" = a brief reference without a full slot. The 4-week callout is a mention; the 2-week run is where full announcement slots begin.

### App & Website Events

All churchwide events (orange column in the [[emmanuel-master-calendar]]) require:
- An event in the ECC app (managed by [[comms-team]])
- An event on the website
- A clear sign-up or register call to action

CCB is locally owned — campus-specific events live there, not in the central comms pipeline.

### Platform Items

**Platform speakers** (any guest or special speaker appearing in a service) must be added to PCO (Planning Center Online) as soon as confirmed. This is an open action item as of May 2026.

**Testimonies and giving stories** should be built into the service flow — not treated as ad hoc additions. Target: last week of each month, alternating stewardship and life-change. See [[180-testimony-videos]] for pipeline detail.

**Baptism as CTA:** Every big rock event should carry a baptism call to action in its follow-through. This is a standing protocol reinforced by Nathan Grams.

### Focus Protection

Avoid stacking competing promotions. When multiple major events overlap, stagger cadence timing to prevent audience fatigue. "Protect the focus of other events."

## Special Variations

### CG Campaign Series (Fall, every year)
- Adds: Connect Group booklet cover + assets, CG videos + bumper
- Timed for Fall Kickoff (September)
- 2024: Love Thy Neighbor | 2025: CG Series | 2026: TBD

### KB Series (Fall, every year)
- Adds: KB engagement-focused elements
- Overlaps with or follows CG Campaign series
- 2024: Because of Eternity | 2025: Tell Me Why

### Christmas Series (December, every year)
- Branded as a seasonal look, not just a series
- Includes: full seasonal look + graphics package, ad campaign, photobooths, banners, Christmas Eve production
- Must be ready by Dec 1 (first Sunday of Christmas series)
- 2024: Road to Bethlehem | 2025: Unto Us

### Easter Series (March/April)
- Full campaign treatment: graphics package + ad campaign + banners + photobooths + testimony videos
- Often leads directly into a new post-Easter series (So You Believe / Family Matters)
- Good Friday service also needs comms support (Communion, simpler)
