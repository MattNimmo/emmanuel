**TLDR:** ECC's 4-tier volunteer leadership framework — Team Member → Leader → Coach → Coordinator. Each level defines a span of care, a training requirement, and a spiritual posture. Being introduced as the Essential Leadership Track at TEC 2026 (Aug 15).

---

## The Framework

Built on the conviction that "leadership advancement reflects both increased responsibility and increased spiritual maturity." Growth Track is the entry point for every level.

| Level | Span of Care | Minimum Training |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Team Member** | Reports to 1 Leader | Growth Track |
| **Leader** | Up to 10 volunteers | Growth Track + Essential |
| **Coach** | Up to 5 Leaders | Growth Track + Essential + Intermediate |
| **Coordinator** | Up to 5 Coaches; reports to Director | Growth Track + Essential + Intermediate + Advanced |

Full definitions, responsibilities, and quotes from the source doc: [[leadership-levels-playbook]].

---

## Context

This framework did not exist publicly prior to 2026. Before TEC, ECC had Growth Track as the on-ramp and informal ministry team structures, but no named tiering system for advancement after that.

Jodi Ruch and Brandi Hammond authored the framework as part of the **Essential Leadership Track** — a follow-up to Growth Track that gives volunteers a visible development ladder. It addresses a real frustration surfaced at TEC 2025: long-tenured volunteers felt sessions were too basic and had no path forward articulated.

---

## First Use

TEC 2026 (Aug 15) is the official launch. Nathan plans to:
1. Introduce the framework in general sessions (vision-level)
2. Use afternoon location breakouts to cast vision for how each level plays out in each ministry context
3. Enable session contributors (high-capacity long-tenured volunteers) to teach within their tier

See [[tec-planning-meeting-06-01]] for format details.

---

## Tensions

- **Basics vs. advanced:** The framework must serve a volunteer who's been there 20 years and someone who showed up last month. TEC's solution: use the framework to *separate* them within a session via the 4-corners format, not combine them into one baseline track.
- **Vision vs. equipping:** Long-tenured volunteers need to feel like contributors, not just recipients. The solution: shoulder-tap them as micro-session teachers or coaches within the breakout, not just attendees.
- **Scaling:** The math works (1 Coordinator can umbrella 250 people), but requires consistent training completion and intentional coaching relationships, neither of which have a built infrastructure yet.
