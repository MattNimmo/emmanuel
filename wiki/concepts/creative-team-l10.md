## Monday Cascade Context

The Creative Team L10 is the third and final meeting in a Monday cascade:

| Meeting | Time | Who | Output |
|---------|------|-----|--------|
| Service Planning | 9:30am | Creative Team + Pastoral Staff | Next 2 Sundays locked |
| Directors Sync | Late morning | Location Pastors + Matt & JC | Direction set; marching orders given |
| **Creative Team L10** | Afternoon | Production + Comms + Worship | Issues solved; to-dos committed |

By L10 time, Matt and JC should arrive with: decisions from Directors Sync, changes from the 9:30am plan, and cascading messages for the team. These feed directly into Headlines and the Issues list.

## The 90-Minute Agenda

| Time | Section | Key Rule |
|------|---------|----------|
| 5 min | Segue | Personal best + ministry best. No problems yet. |
| 5 min | Scorecard | On track or off track per metric. Off-track → Issues list. |
| 5 min | Rock Review | On track or off track per quarterly priority. One sentence max. |
| 5 min | Headlines | One sentence each. Cascading messages delivered here. |
| 5 min | To-Do Review | Done or not done. Target: 90% completion weekly. |
| **60 min** | **IDS** | **Identify, Discuss, Solve. This is the meeting.** |
| 5 min | Conclude | Recap to-dos (owners confirm verbally). Cascading messages. Rate 1–10. |

---

## IDS — The Heart of It

Everything before IDS is designed to take exactly 25 minutes so IDS gets a full hour.

- **Identify:** Rank the issues list 1–5 by priority. Name the real issue — not the symptom. ("Graphics were late" = symptom. "No clear handoff between sermon prep and design" = issue.)
- **Discuss:** Each person speaks once. Facilitator cuts off the second lap.
- **Solve:** Decision, to-do, or deliberate table until X. Owner named. Issue closed.

Solving 1–3 issues deeply beats skimming 10. Unsolved issues re-rank next week.

|--------|-------|--------|
| Service elements locked by Wednesday EOD | Production Lead | 100% |
| Worship setlist + chord charts published | Worship Lead | By Tuesday EOD |

| Open creative requests (intake → assigned) | Comms Lead | ≤5 in queue |

Metrics to add as the team matures: first-time visitor experience rating, social engagement rate, on-time delivery % per role.

## What Makes This Work

The non-negotiables (from EOS):

1. **Same time, same day, every week.** Moving it signals it's not a priority.
2. **Same agenda, same order.** Don't add sections; don't reorder.
3. **Drop, don't debate.** Scorecard/Rocks/Headlines items that need discussion go to Issues — they don't get discussed in-section.
4. **Solve, not just discuss.** Every issue gets a clear next action with an owner before moving on.
5. **Rate it.** Anything under 8 gets a sentence of why. That's how the meeting improves.

## Why This Fits ECC Creative

The current Monday meeting (as seen Apr 29) is essentially an unstructured version of this — walking through upcoming Sundays by department, surfacing issues. The L10 structure gives that existing instinct a frame that:

- Forces prioritization of issues (instead of solving whatever comes up first)
- Creates accountability for to-dos week over week (90% target)
- Builds a weekly scorecard that surfaces capacity and process problems before they blow up

The scorecard is the biggest unlock — it makes the team's delivery rhythm visible without requiring a separate status meeting.
