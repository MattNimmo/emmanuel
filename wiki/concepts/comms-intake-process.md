**TLDR:** ECC's comms team has historically lacked a formal intake process, which creates a recurring pattern of process bypasses, absorbed work, and requests arriving via pastoral authority rather than through a proper channel. Matt and JC are now the primary filter, and a ClickUp-based intake form is being built to replace the informal system that broke down when Audrey left.

---

## The Problem

Before Audrey left, she served as the de facto gatekeeper for comms requests. Her departure left no filter. The result was immediate: two significant process bypass incidents occurred within weeks (see [[comms-team-meeting-04-13]] for full detail).

### The Bypass Pattern

1. A requester contacts someone on the comms team directly
2. The team member responds with a question, alternative, or pushback
3. Silence — no reply
4. The same request re-arrives via a pastor (Nate, Joey, Darren, etc.) with implied or explicit authority
5. The team member can no longer push back — the project happens on unclear terms

This pattern is demoralizing and creates bad project conditions from the start.

### The Side-Project Pattern

Requests framed as "a fun little project" or "a brain break" that are actually:
- Non-comms work (decorating rooms, making thank-you cards, putting clocks on walls)
- Urgent enough to interrupt actual projects
- Often coming from well-meaning people who don't understand the team's scope

## Current State (Apr 2026)

- **Matt and JC are now the filter.** Requests should go to them first; they route or decline
- **ClickUp intake form in progress** — [[jess-porter]] is already building questions into the intake flow to clarify scope, deadline, and context before a request reaches the team
- **Comms Resource Channel** being resurrected by Jess Porter — gives other departments self-serve brand assets (hex codes, fonts, stock photos, templates) so they don't need to come to comms for low-level design tasks

## Design Principles for the Intake System

These surfaced from the team meeting discussion:

1. **One path in.** All requests go through the intake form or Matt/JC — not directly to individual team members
2. **Scope clarity upfront.** Is this comms work? Is it P0–P1 priority? If not, it gets redirected or declined before anyone starts
3. **Deadline required.** No deadline = no start. The KB video experience showed what happens when turnaround is assumed but never stated
4. **Conflict resolution owned by Matt/JC.** When two requests arrive simultaneously and compete for capacity, Matt and JC own the tiebreak — they don't escalate; they decide
5. **"Inspiration is not permission."** (Term coined by Dr. Lori in a later conversation.) When Pastor Nate shares an idea, that is not a green light. Matt/JC confirm whether it's a directive, a vision item for later, or just a thought

---

## What Redirects Instead of Comms

Per [[jess-porter]]'s observation: many requests that come to central comms could and should be handled at location level:
- Canva for simple graphics
- Amazon for supplies
- Location admins for internal event logistics

The Comms Resource Channel is the enabler — giving departments the tools to self-serve appropriately.
