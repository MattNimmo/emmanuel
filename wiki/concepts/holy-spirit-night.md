**TLDR:** Annual evening event at SLP focused on extended altar/prayer time; 2026 edition planned with a compressed format (shorter worship, tighter mid-service, 25-min message) and intentional kids programming. Date not confirmed in transcript.

## 2026 Format

| Element | 2026 Plan | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Worship | 18–20 min | Shorter than prior years (30–40 min) |
| Mid-service | Offering + transition only | No extra elements |
| Message | ~25 min | Compressed; context provided in prior 2 Sundays of "What About the Holy Spirit" |
| Altars/prayer | ~45 min planned | "Goes as long as it goes" |
| Staff prayer call | 5:15 PM | Door one |
| Doors / check-in | 5:30–5:40 PM | Kids check-in 5:30, classrooms open 5:40 |
| Service start | 6:00 PM |  |

## Why the Compressed Message?
The prior two Sundays (May 17 + following Sunday) cover Holy Spirit context in the "What About?" series. The 25-min message serves guests and those who missed the context — not a full teaching moment.

## Kids Ministry (Manny)
- **Up to 2nd grade:** Standard childcare
- **2nd–5th grade:** Holy Spirit Night lesson — intentional programming with expectation that God will move in elementary-age kids too
- Families and "patients" (?) encouraged to bring kids; communicate expectation in promotions
- Check-in: 5:30 PM; classrooms open: 5:40 PM
- **Open items:** Kids suite for serving parents TBD; cross-location staffing required (Nate direction: not SLP's burden alone)
