**TLDR:** A four-tier priority framework for evaluating all communications requests at [[emmanuel-christian-center]]. P0 is Sunday-service events only. P1 is growth-driving and major non-Sunday strategic events. P2 is member-serving work done when capacity allows. P3 is not a comms team item — ministries are equipped to handle it themselves. Framework finalized 2026-04-21; see [[pastor-nate-priority-questions]] for the full Q&A.

---

## The Framework

### P0 — Non-Negotiable. Always yes.
**Rule:** Sunday-service events only. Full production. Planned in advance with lead time. No trade-offs made against these.

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| Sermon Series (all) | Year-round, 8–9x/year |
| Easter | March/April |
| Christmas | December |
| Super Sunday | February |
| KB Catalytic Weekends (×2) | January + November |
| Miracle Offering | November |
| Fall Kickoff | September |

> The defining test for P0: **Does it happen on a Sunday and drive the primary service experience?** If yes, it's P0.

---

### P1 — High Priority. Strong yes.
**Rule:** Growth-driving work, Sunday-adjacent content, and major non-Sunday strategic events. These get a yes in almost all circumstances. In a genuine capacity crunch, P1 items may require trade-offs against each other — resolved by Matt + JC based on context (see tiebreaker below).

**Protected P1 exceptions (never deprioritized below P1 regardless of capacity):**
- **Baptism** — always protected
- **Growth Track** — always protected

**Major non-Sunday events:**
| Event | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Sisterhood Spring + Fall | Large standalone events; can move down within P1 if context demands |
| Worship Night | Full graphics package, promo video, recording + production |
| 21 Days of Prayer & Fasting | Public-facing; strategic January campaign |
| Kids Camp | Public-facing; growth-oriented |

**Sunday-adjacent and recurring:**
| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| Department content that plays on Sunday | KB 5-spot video, ELC promo shown in service — Sunday placement elevates regardless of origin |
| Weekly social posts | When attached to Sunday, a P0 event, or a strategic series |
| Recurring machine-keeping commitments | Weekly email, central announcement slides, sermon clips |

> The defining test for P1: **Is it growth-driving, public-facing, or does it touch Sunday even if it originates from a department?**

---

### P2 — Do when capacity allows. Outsource when not.
**Rule:** Legitimate comms work, but member-serving rather than growth-driving. Not Sunday-facing. When the team is full, these are routed to a vetted third-party vendor. Priority within P2 is determined by ministry impact and deadline.

| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| Connect Group series videos | Produced for small groups, not Sunday — borderline P1 due to CG Campaign strategy, but internal audience keeps it P2 |
| ELC promotional materials | Externally facing but not Sunday-driving |
| Print collateral for smaller events | Brand-consistent materials for ministry events below P1 |
| Department-specific design | Not tied to Sunday; no growth driver |

> The defining test for P2: **Is it a real comms team responsibility, but the audience is primarily existing members and it doesn't touch Sunday?**

---

### P3 — Not a comms item. Empower and redirect.
**Rule:** Internal department-to-volunteer communications. The comms team's role here is equipping, not executing. Ministries are trained to handle these themselves using Canva, brand templates, and guidelines provided by the team.

**Response to P3 requests:**
- Provide a Canva template or brand asset
- Walk the requester through how to produce it themselves
- Teach the skill — don't own the task

> The defining test for P3: **Is this a message from a ministry department to its own volunteers or internal team?** If yes, it's not a comms item.

---

## The Tiebreaker

When two requests compete for the same capacity:
1. Higher tier always wins (P0 over P1, P1 over P2)
2. Within the same tier: **Sunday-related and externally focused items win**
3. Within P1 specifically, when two events collide, evaluate in this order:
   - **Sunday-related** beats non-Sunday
   - **Growth-driving** beats member-serving
   - **External reach** beats internal audience
   - Remaining ties → **Matt and Joncarlos decide** based on year and context; no escalation to Nate required
4. In heavy P1 seasons, **scaling back production quality** is a valid alternative to outsourcing — approached situationally, not by preset rule

---

## Outsourcing Triggers

When a P2 item cannot be absorbed by the team, it goes to a third-party vendor. Candidates for outsourcing:
- Print materials (light boxes, merch, seat backs, welcome cards)
- Event photography not requiring brand voice consistency
- Lower-priority video production (outside events)
- Department-specific design not tied to Sunday

P3 items are never outsourced by the comms team — they are self-served by the requesting ministry.

---

## Late Request Handling

A late request for a P1 deliverable is not the same ask as a well-resourced one. When lead time is insufficient:
- **Matt and Joncarlos decide** whether the request stays on the team's plate or gets outsourced
- No automatic downgrade by priority tier — judgment call at their level
