**TLDR:** "180" videos are short testimony stories — people whose lives have done a 180° turn because of their faith. The entire comms team and Pastor Nate independently named this as ECC's highest-value content type. They are currently inconsistent and ad-hoc; the team wants to build them into a regular, scalable content rhythm.

---

## What They Are

Short-form video testimonials capturing how someone's life has changed through faith at Emmanuel. Named after the concept of a 180° transformation. Past examples include "Daniel's 180." Theologically grounded in: *"They overcame by the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony"* (Rev. 12:11).

## Proposed Two-Tier Format

| Tier | Format | Use Case |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Quick / Reflex** | Good mic + camera, not phone-quality but fast turnaround (hours to days) | As soon as a story surfaces; regular social content |
| **Mini-doc** | Travel to location, B-roll, deeply produced | Occasional; high-impact series or Sunday features |

The long mini-doc format is powerful but consumes too much capacity to do frequently. The quick tier is designed to make story capture a reflex, not a project.

---

## Proposed Pipeline

1. **Story intake:** Anyone (pastor, LP, team member) who hears a compelling testimony inputs it into a shared database — name, location, story snapshot, contact info
2. **Follow-up tracking:** Database marks whether the person has been contacted
3. **Location rep routing:** Contact goes to the location's LP or person who submitted the story
4. **Format decision:** Matt + JC decide quick vs. mini-doc based on story depth and current capacity

### Baptism Integration (Jess's Idea)
At [[jess-porter]]'s prior church: baptism prep = pastoral interview that captured testimony → 30-second edited clip played during the baptism service. This creates a built-in, repeatable pipeline. Proposed for ECC.
