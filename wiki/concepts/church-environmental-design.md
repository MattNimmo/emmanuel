**TLDR:** [[sara-wescott]]'s vision for transforming ECC's physical spaces into environments that are spiritually meaningful, visually intentional, and shareable on social media. The current state — blank walls and outdated Echo-era artwork — is a missed opportunity for both congregation connection and organic marketing.

---

## Current State Problems

- Many blank walls across campuses, especially at SLP
- **Echo artwork** — Sara describes it as "Soviet-looking"; it was part of an old ECC brand era that never fully landed. Needs to be removed and replaced
- Few Instagrammable moments — people share beautiful/meaningful spaces; ECC currently has few of these

---

## Sara's Vision

### Porch Building (SLP)
- The porch renovation has been underway for ~a year
- Sara wants a single, clear message on the back of the porch building: **"A House of Prayer for All Nations"** — white Helvetica font
- Visible from the parking lot (Door 4 / Osborne) and from University
- She has had a mockup ready for over a year — needs final approval and execution
- Reference inspiration: Saints Church in Brooklyn — giant black brick wall, "Every saint has a past, every sinner has a future" — no other context needed

### Testimony Walls / Portrait Displays
- Portraits of congregants serving, with their testimony beside them
- Rotating TVs in central wall spaces (so content can be updated)
- Locket fringes or Polaroid walls where attendees can add themselves
- QR code for "share your story" form embedded at display points (JC's idea)

### Interactive Elements
- "Every person at Emmanuel had a part in this" — Sara wants installations where congregants can say "I helped build that" or "That's my name on that thing"
- Example: Gratitude wall from past seasons — post-it notes still cherished by volunteers; now lives in a jar in Maple Grove's "Our Gratitude" corner

### Sidewalk / Outdoor Space
- Let kids write chalk welcomes on sidewalks before Sunday services (Maple Grove already did this successfully — "Welcome to Emmanuel" in chalk drawn by kids)
- Street-level text or paving messages

---

## Strategic Case

- People share beautiful spaces organically — this is free reach
- Anytime Maple Grove changes décor, new families photograph it every single Sunday (Jess Porter confirmed: three-screen installations, light boxes, etc. all get photographed)
- These aren't just aesthetic improvements — they tell the congregation's story in physical form
