**TLDR:** Emmanuel's Production Team — internally called the "Tech Team" — is a volunteer-driven ministry led by a Staff Service Producer. It runs all live audio, video, lighting, and broadcast coordination across ECC's multi-campus weekend services. Target service length: 70–80 minutes. Governing philosophy: "Services, not shows."

---

## Leadership

- **Service Producer (Staff-led):** [[josh-patterson]] — production lead at SLP; signed the Production Handbook; oversees all broadcast producing roles
- **Live Video Director (Staff-led):** Oversees all video directing roles; partners with Service Producer and Lighting
- Operates under the broader vision of Lead Pastors [[nate-ruch]] and [[jodi-ruch]]

---

## Mission & Identity

Role: facilitate a life-changing Weekend Experience at all locations by providing an engaging and supportive production experience that points people to Jesus.

### Three Identity Values
1. **Dependable** — show up; do what you said you'd do
2. **Approachable** — never project "leave me alone"; intensity never dictates behavior
3. **Flexible** — details arrive late; adapt and strategize afterward

---

## Vision Principles

- "Vibrant, Spirit-filled worship experiences" (Pastor Nate Ruch's definition of the goal)
- Service experience begins in the parking lot — First Impressions → lobby music → sanctuary
- **Target service length: 70–80 minutes**
- "The sermon isn't over until the response has been sung."
- **"Services, not shows."**
- Minimize distractions; inspire people toward the Gospel through lyrics and teaching

## Role Hierarchy

Two tracks reporting to the Service Producer:

### Broadcast Producing Track (Service Producer leads)
| Role | Core Function |
|------|--------------|
| Broadcast Timekeeper | Manages timing device; generates the element-by-element time data used in weekly service sheets |
| Inter-Location Comms | Creates time stamps as "play buttons" for locations to sync to the broadcast stream; communicates deviations |
| Stage Manager | All platform gear movement; wears headset; "bring out platform pieces at the correct time, every time" |
| Makeup Artist | Preps communicators in Pastor's Hub before services; powders Pastor Nate and speakers list |
| FOH Audio | House mix; in-ear monitors; **88–94 dB target during full energy moments**; collaborates with WL and MD |
| BOH Audio | Online broadcast mix; Dante Virtual Sound Card + WAVES Sound Grid; instrument replacement software |
| Lighting Designer | Programs lighting per Planning Center service run sheet; dynamic cues by song structure; **ready 1 day in advance** |
| Lighting Tech | Executes Designer's cues live; monitors haze ("like smoking ribs, low and slow"); adapts to unannounced altar time |

### Video Directing Track (Live Video Director leads)
| Role | Core Function |
|------|--------------|
| Message Notes | Inputs scriptures and graphics for the message in real time; seen at ALL locations and online forever; reviewed with Service Producer or Comms Lead before concluding |
| I-Mag Director | Drives Image Magnification screens for in-room audience; 75% Camera 3 / 25% Camera 2 during message |
| Graphic Operator | Operates ProPresenter; countdown starts 5 min before service; pre-service loop 30+ min before |
| Shading | Adjusts iris/color levels for all cameras; white balance before every service; formats record drives on arrival |
| Cameras 2&3 | In-service cameras; Cam 2 = head-to-toe (message default); Cam 3 = head-to-waist (message default) |
| Cameras 1&4 | In-service cameras; same smooth-movement and focus-check standards |

## Operational Standards

### Service Producer's Pre-Service Checklist
Before every service, five questions:
1. Do we have the **vision** of how to pull it off?
2. Do we have the **people** to pull it off?
3. Do we have the **content** to pull it off?
4. Do we have the **time** to pull it off?
5. Do we have the **experience** to pull it off — i.e., rehearsal?

### Rehearsal Culture
- Dress rehearsal is treated as first service
- Lighting must be programmed and ready at least one day in advance
- Video Director plans camera capture approach days before, not the day-of
- All camera operators run cameras during rehearsal to get shot direction from Director

### Multi-Campus Broadcast Workflow
- Broadcast campus produces the live service
- Inter-Location Comms creates time stamps during dress rehearsal and services
- Locations receive stamps via the "chat"; stamps act as play buttons to sync their stream
- When broadcast deviates from plan, Inter-Location Comms proactively communicates to all locations

### Key Software & Tools
- **Planning Center** — service run sheet; used by Graphic Operator, Lighting Designer, Makeup Artist (speakers list)
- **ProPresenter** — worship lyrics and message graphics (Graphic Operator)
- **Ableton** — click/track management (MD role in Worship Team, not Production)
- **Dante Virtual Sound Card** — audio routing (FOH + BOH)
- **WAVES Sound Grid** — online broadcast audio (BOH)

## Camera Shot Philosophy

| Context | In-Room | Online |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Worship | Leading vocalists / instrumentalists; lyrics visible | Dynamic movement; wider room shots |
| Message | Head-to-toe / head-to-knee; present pastor to the room | Wider, atmospheric; present the room to the viewer |
