## Approach and Framing

The series was described in planning as "heavy" — Revelation's letters address sin patterns, idolatry, and spiritual compromise directly. Week 1 (Ephesus) is load-bearing: it must establish the interpretive lens and earn enough trust to carry the audience through the harder weeks.

**Design principle:** root each church in its specific ancient historical and geographic context before moving to contemporary application. The Twin Cities is the explicit landing zone — not a generic American church.

Nate Ruch visited all seven sites in Turkey in early 2026. He has firsthand photos, videos, and recorded guide lectures from each location. These are the primary creative source material for the series.

**Series subtitle (proposed):** "The Future Is Ancient"

Key historical facts from the Turkey trip:
- **Laodicea:** Excavated in the last 5 years. Hidden house church found (ichthys-marked corridor). ~50 years after the "lukewarm" letter, the church was hiding from persecution — proof they repented and became bold.
- **Ephesus:** Second most excavated city in the world after Cairo. Library of Ephesus = one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
- **Pergamum:** Theater on a mountainside; 25,000 seats; steeper than Red Rocks. A martyr's site.
- **Philadelphia:** Only two stone posts remain in a modern city. Little visual material; message depends on the letter.
- **Colossae:** Not yet excavated — just a hill.

The guide (Jeremy) gave on-site teachings. Nate has audio recordings. This material is the creative foundation for bumpers, illustrations, and social content.

## Creative Direction (Decided — May 20, 2026)

### Platform / Stage
- Existing white drapes crumpled/scrunched to look like rustic stone columns; uplit for ancient texture.
- Gobo projections: Turkey imagery shot onto draped columns.
- Vertical emphasis retained; the format already reads as column-like.

### Lobby Photo Backdrop
- Each location gets: prop columns (styrofoam), Ephesus Library arch motif, palm trees, greenery.
- Purpose: selfie/photo moment anchoring attendees in the series.
- Center screen visual rotates weekly to match each specific church's geographic identity.

### Promo Video
- Style: narrative voiceover; **not** a travel documentary.
- Frame: "This is our history. Jesus is still speaking to churches."
- Content: Turkey stills/video + Anoka amphitheater (possible local shoot) + audio from Jeremy's guide lectures.
- Possible two-narrator approach: historical voice + pastoral voice.
- Reference: ECC's prior Joshua series intro (~90 sec narrative + "join us" structure).

### Tour Poster Concept
- Series branded like a rock concert tour: list of upcoming "cities" (churches) with dates.
- Runs on lobby screens and in the free-service loop all summer.

### Social Media
- **After-service takeaway interviews:** Brief (20 sec), candid, diverse ages/backgrounds.
  - Question: "What's your takeaway from today?" or "If God wrote you a letter, what would it say?"
  - Pre-brief 2–3 people; also capture organic responses.
  - Run mid-week throughout the series.
- **Deeper dives:** Week-of content featuring the week's speaker going deeper on the historical context.
- Frame: "Excavating deeper into the message."
