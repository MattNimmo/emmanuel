**TLDR:** Emmanuel's annual all-volunteer leadership conference, bringing together staff and ministry volunteers from all four campuses for a full Saturday of worship, vision-casting, and ministry training. **2026: August 15.** Format mirrors 2025 with one major addition — Nathan Grams' Essential Leadership Track anchoring the afternoon location breakouts.

---

## What It Is

TEC is ECC's largest internal volunteer event of the year. Audience: all ministry volunteers and staff across ELK, LV, MG, and SLP. Purpose: alignment across locations, ministry training, and team cohesion before the fall season.

It is not a public event. It is held on a Saturday in mid-August, positioned as the launch ramp into the fall ministry calendar.

## The Essential Leadership Track (New in 2026)

Nathan Grams is introducing a formal 4-tier volunteer pipeline — **Team Member → Leader → Coach → Coordinator** — as the content backbone for afternoon location breakouts. This is the first public introduction of the framework, which functions as a follow-up to Growth Track and gives long-tenured volunteers a visible development ladder.

The afternoon breakout format:
- Vision-cast the whole framework (why levels matter, what they mean)
- Location pastor facilitates; volunteers break into subtopics by level using a "4 corners" format
- High-capacity veterans invited to teach 15-min micro-sessions within breakouts (not just attend)

Full details: [[leadership-pipeline]], [[leadership-levels-playbook]], [[tec-planning-meeting-06-01]].
