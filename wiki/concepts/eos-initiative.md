**TLDR:** ECC is formally adopting a custom organizational operating system ("Emmanuel Operating System") based on the EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) framework, facilitated by board member Andy Ballew. Running concurrently: an accelerated search for an Executive Pastor (XP). Both are expected to be operational within 2026–2027.

---

## Background

When former Executive Pastor Ben left, Nate Ruch absorbed both Lead Pastor and XP responsibilities. Over several years, the dual role proved unsustainable: decision-making slowed, accountability became unclear, and Nate's capacity for vision work eroded. In December 2025, Nate began praying about a solution and discussing it with the board.

Board consensus by early 2026: ECC needs both a formal organizational operating system **and** an Executive Pastor. Andy Ballew volunteered to consult on the operating system work.

*Prior attempts:* ECC went through StratOPS (a Saddleback-origin operating system) in a prior season. That work was not fully implemented. EOS explicitly builds on that foundation rather than discarding it.

## EOS Framework — Six Components

| Component | Status | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| **Vision** | In development | VTO (Vision/Traction Organizer) first draft in progress; core values being redefined |
| **People** | Not started | Accountability chart (not org chart) — one owner per deliverable |
| **Data** | Not started | Scorecards and KPIs; church context makes this harder than manufacturing |
| **Issues** | Not started | L10 meeting structure for surfacing/resolving issues |
| **Process** | Not started | Documented best practices; standardized "how we do things here" |
| **Traction** | Not started | 90-day rocks; meeting cadence |

**Target:** All six components operational within 6 months of May 2026 (by ~November 2026).

---

## Key Concepts

**VTO (Vision/Traction Organizer):** The primary EOS planning artifact. Defines core values, core focus/mission, 3-year picture, 10-year target, and target market. Cascades down to department-level VTOs (Worship VTO, Comms VTO, etc.).

**Accountability Chart:** Replaces the org chart. Every function has one accountable person — not a committee, not shared. Named explicitly so finger-pointing becomes impossible.

**L10 Meeting (Level Ten):** Structured weekly leadership meeting. Goal: attendees rate it 8–10/10. Antiseptic and data-driven at its core; ECC will adapt with a 5-minute relational open. Not all meetings become L10 meetings — creative sessions, brainstorming, etc. retain their own format.

**Rocks:** 90-day organizational priorities — the most important thing to move forward in a given quarter.

**Visionary / Integrator:** (from *Rocket Fuel*) The visionary (Nate) provides direction, dreams, and spiritual leadership. The integrator (future XP) executes, manages the operating system, and connects the dots. These roles must be clearly separated.

## Named Organizational Problems This Addresses

From Andy Ballew's diagnostic in the May 20 meeting:
- Clunky systems (re-entering contact info 13 years in)
- Firefighter culture — heroic last-minute execution instead of planned delivery
- Inconsistent location experience (first impressions, kids' ministry, worship)
- Thin bench strength behind key leaders
- Unclear central vs. location accountability
- Silos: each team has its own mini-system, arrows pulling in different directions
- Passive-aggressive water-cooler culture; end-arounds to Nate
- "Low trust organization" — culture of honor sometimes suppresses honest feedback
