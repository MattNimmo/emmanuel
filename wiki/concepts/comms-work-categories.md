**TLDR:** Five categories that describe the nature of any comms deliverable or request. Used to quickly determine priority, assign lead time, and make capacity trade-offs. Every deliverable gets at least one tag. A single project (e.g. Easter) can generate deliverables across multiple categories.

---

## The Five Categories

### 1. Sunday Experience
The baseline weekly product. Content consumed during the Sunday service itself — every series, every week.

**Defining test:** Is this content used during a Sunday service?

**Typical deliverables:**
- Sermon series graphics package (series graphic, speaker background, lower thirds, social post)
- Bumper video
- Screen support (announcement slides, special offering, lower thirds)
- Weekly social graphic adaptations from series art

**Priority mapping:** P0 core. Always planned, always resourced.

**Notes:** KB Catalytic Weekends are primarily Sunday Experience (service elements, engagement cards shown on Sunday) with some Ministry Event elements (KB video, partner promo). The Sunday service treatment stays Sunday Experience.

---

### 2. Invitational Sunday
Specific high-production Sundays built to bring new people through the door. These get elevated, campaign-level treatment stacked on top of Sunday Experience deliverables — ad campaigns, banners, photobooths, invite materials, elevated graphics.

**Defining test:** Would you hand someone an invite card for this Sunday?

**Events:**
| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| Easter | March/April |
| Christmas | December |
| Super Sunday | February |
| Fall Kickoff | September |

**Typical additional deliverables (beyond Sunday Experience):**
- Ad campaign (digital + print)
- Elevated/seasonal graphics package
- Banners (all locations)
- Photobooth design + ordering
- Invite cards / pop-up event listings
- Promo video or service stingers

**Priority mapping:** P0. Maximum lead time required.

**Notes:** Fall Kickoff spans both Invitational Sunday and Ministry Event — the Sunday service is Sunday Experience; the event package (merch, graphics campaign) is Invitational Sunday. Tag both when applicable.

---

### 3. Ministry Event
Standalone event production. A specific date or weekend with a defined deliverable set. Not a Sunday service. Serves the congregation or a specific ministry community.

**Defining test:** Is this a standalone event (not a Sunday service) that needs its own production treatment?

**Examples:**
| Event | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Sisterhood Spring + Fall | Largest standalone events; top of P1 |
| Worship Night | Full production + recording |
| 21 Days of Prayer & Fasting | January; spiritual formation; public-facing |
| Kids Camp | Next Gen; promo-focused |
| Team Emmanuel Conference | Volunteer-facing; August |
| Serve Day | Community-facing; lighter production |

**Typical deliverables:**
- Event graphics package
- Promo video
- Merch design + ordering
- Photo shoot
- Social campaign tied to the event
- Announcement slide (central announcement = always included)

**Priority mapping:** P1 (major, public-facing events) or P2 (smaller, member-serving events).

---

### 4. Recurring Content
Always-on cadence work that keeps the church's presence and communication rhythm running week to week. This work is never "done" — it ships on a fixed schedule regardless of what else is happening. Skipping it compounds.

**Defining test:** Does this repeat on a fixed schedule and exist independently of any single event?

**Deliverables:**
| Task | Cadence | Owner |
|------|---------|-------|
| Emmanuel MN social posts | 2–3x/week | Sara Wescott *(confirmation pending)* |
| Weekly email | Weekly | Designer or lead |
| Sermon clips creation + posting | Weekly | Videographer |
| Sunday photo coverage + editing | Weekly | Photographer |
| Sunday message video editing + upload | Weekly | Videographer |
| Central announcement slides | As needed / weekly | Designer |
| Lobby TV management | Weekly | Misc |
| Dr. Nate Ruch book campaign socials | TBD cadence | TBD *(more context forthcoming)* |

**Priority mapping:** P1 baseline. These protect the machine. Deprioritized only in genuine crisis.

---

### 5. Department Support
Work originating from a specific ministry department for their own audience. Narrower reach. Not Sunday-facing unless explicitly placed there — in which case it gets elevated to Sunday Experience or Invitational Sunday.

**Defining test:** Did a department originate this for their own ministry audience rather than the full congregation?

**Departments and typical work:**
| Department | Typical Deliverables |
|------------|---------------------|
| ELC | Social posts, video production, event graphics, merch |
| SHL Podcast | Recording, editing, thumbnail, social cuts |
| Kingdom Builders (non-Sunday) | Print materials, partner slides, non-service video |
| Dr. Nate Ruch | Website management, design, non-book social |
| Connect Groups | CG series videos (internal; borderline P1 due to strategic CG Campaign, but internal audience keeps it P2) |
| Worship (non-Worship Night) | Music video, album cover, non-event social |

**Priority mapping:** P2 (legitimate comms work, done when capacity allows, outsourced when not) or P3 (internal volunteer communications — empower and redirect, not a comms team item).

**P3 redirect policy:** If a department request is internal communication from a ministry to its own volunteer team, the comms team equips them to do it — Canva template, brand guidelines, brief training. Teach the skill; don't own the task.

## How Categories Map to Priority Tiers

| Category | Typical Priority | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| Sunday Experience | P0 | Core product; always resourced |
| Invitational Sunday | P0 | Max production; max lead time |
| Ministry Event | P1 → P2 | Depends on scale and public reach |
| Recurring Content | P1 | Protect the machine |
| Department Support | P2 → P3 | Outsource or redirect when full |

---

## Using Categories in ClickUp

Every task in ClickUp should carry a **Category** custom field (dropdown, single-select). One project may generate tasks across multiple categories — tag each task individually, not the project as a whole.

**Example — Easter:**
| Task | Category |
|------|---------|
| Easter series graphics package | Sunday Experience |
| Easter bumper video | Sunday Experience |
| Easter ad campaign assets | Invitational Sunday |
| Easter banners design + ordering | Invitational Sunday |
| Easter testimony video | Invitational Sunday |
| Easter photobooth design | Invitational Sunday |

This distinction matters for filtering the Workload view by category — it shows you not just who is over capacity, but *what kind of work* is causing the overload.
