**TLDR:** ECC's established cadence for promoting churchwide events: 4 weeks out (save the date + app CTA), 2 weeks of central announcements, and a Monday email echo at each stage. Big rocks get 3 announcement slots; churchwide events get 2. Comms owns the app; CCB is locally owned.

---

## The Cadence

| Timing | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **4 weeks out** | "Save the date" mention in service + call to action in the app; Monday email echoes the churchwide with the 4-week callout |
| **2 weeks out** | Central announcements begin — full announcement slot(s) run through the event |

---

## Announcement Slot Counts

A standing guideline (not a new rule — existing practice):

| Event Type | Announcement Slots |
|------------|-------------------|
| **Former big rocks** (KB, Miracle Offering, Easter, etc.) | 3 |
| **Churchwide events** (general church-wide promotions) | 2 |

**Distinction:** An "announcement" is a full dedicated slot. A "mention" is a brief reference without a full slot. The 4-week callout is a mention; the 2-week run is where full announcements begin.

---

## App & Website Protocol

- **Churchwide events in the app** are managed and updated by the [[comms-team]]
- All events in the **orange column** of the [[emmanuel-master-calendar]] require:
  - An event created in the app
  - An event created on the website
  - A clear call to action (sign up, register, etc.)
- **CCB (Church Community Builder)** is locally owned — each campus manages its own database independently

---

## Monday Email

The Monday email echoes the churchwide calendar at each cadence stage:
- At 4 weeks out: includes the save-the-date mention and app CTA
- During the 2-week run: carries the full announcement details

---

## Focus Protection

"Protect the focus of other events" — don't stack competing promotions. When major events overlap in the calendar, cadence timing may need to be staggered to avoid audience fatigue and message dilution.
