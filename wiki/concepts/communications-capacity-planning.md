Build a ClickUp view that shows:
- What the comms team is working on at any given time
- How many hours each task will consume
- Whether the team has capacity or is over-allocated
- When to say no / defer / outsource

## 85% Capacity Threshold — SMART Goal

At the Apr 14 Central Meeting, Matt stated a formal SMART goal for the comms team:

> **"Reduce or reprioritize work anytime the team is above 85% capacity, so we don't go beyond that threshold."**

This operationalizes the capacity framework as a trigger, not just a planning tool. At 85%:
- Matt and JC actively review the queue
- Lower-priority items get deferred or outsourced
- The threshold prevents the team from absorbing just one more thing until health degrades

85% of ~57 hrs/week effective = **~48 hrs/week** is the ceiling before reprioritization kicks in. At current baseline (~15.5 hrs recurring + project load), the team will exceed 85% during any heavy season (Jan, Apr, Sep, Nov, Dec). The trigger matters most in those windows.
