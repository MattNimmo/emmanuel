## Strengths & Character

- **Principled on quality:** Raised the KB partner video logo question as an integrity issue — genuinely didn't understand the intent, not complaining; resolved constructively when JC explained the rationale
- **Clear communicator:** When he disagrees with a project direction, he says so and proposes an alternative. When he holds a boundary (e.g., the JoviConnector release schedule), he holds it
- **Built a sound team at Elk River** despite having a different operator every single week for four weeks running — a quietly impressive piece of team-building under pressure
- **Self-aware about contracting:** When working on KB generosity videos as a contractor, said he would not have worked with that organization again under the conditions given (unclear deadlines, conflicting directions, no turnaround communication) if it weren't his home church

## Pain Points

- **Process bypasses** — the two clearest examples from the Apr 13 meeting (Keneally podcast, JoviConnector videos); both followed the pattern: Zac weighs in → silence → request re-arrives via a pastor. Pattern started immediately after Audrey left.
- **Absorbed responsibilities** — taken on tasks from departed roles at maintenance-level capacity, not ownership-level capacity. He names this as "nobody is happy" territory — the work exists but can't be done well.
- **Draining projects without clear ownership:** The KB generosity video experience (contractor) — shoot confirmed, turnaround deadline never communicated, conflicting instructions from Darren and Neil on the same day about whether it was being used that Sunday
