**TLDR:** Sara Westcott (official spelling per HR records; "Wescott" used in prior wiki entries) is ECC's graphic designer on the comms team — the sole internal design resource. She covers all graphics packages, print, merch, social templates, and Sunday photo coverage. She is highly creative and quality-driven; her highest-energy work is hands-on, concept-through-execution projects. Her biggest drains are absorbed administrative work and being treated as a catch-all for non-design requests.

## What Energizes Her

- **Practical-shoot series packages** — buying props, building something physical, then layering design on top (Easter cross from broken Goodwill vases = her cited example of ideal work)
- **Merch** — especially when given creative freedom (ELC/NYC merch her favorite); her largest single time commitment (~30–60 hrs per project) and her #1 energizing work
- **Book cover design** — specifically the *Reaching into Your Future* cover; working directly with Pastor Nate felt special and personal
- **Church environmental design** — Sara cares deeply about the physical spaces of ECC; see [[church-environmental-design]]

## What Drains Her

- Absorbed work from Neil and Audrey's departures: app management, website updates, ordering/logistics
- Random non-design requests: decorating rooms, making "bougie" thank you cards, miscellaneous requests from Olivia and others
- Receiving social media strategy input from leadership without having the capacity to execute it well — feels like "stab, twist"
- The social media bulletin-board feeling — posts that get 25 likes don't feel worth the energy
- Social media without a full-time dedicated person is not sustainable at the level being expected
