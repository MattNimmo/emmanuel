## Ministries & Departments

### Weekend Services
The primary weekly touchpoint. Two services per campus (except Lakeville). Sermon series drive all comms output — see [[sermon-series-comms-workflow]].

### Connect Groups (CG)
Small groups that form around sermon series. A CG Campaign launches each Fall (September) with a dedicated sermon series, booklets, and a CG recruitment push. Second CG launch in Spring (February-ish, tied to a series).

### Kingdom Builders (KB)
Above-and-beyond giving program for those who tithe consistently. Funds go to global missions and community impact. Two **KB Catalytic Weekends** per year (January and November) are non-negotiable comms events — include engagement cards, KB launch videos, and 5-spot spotlight segments in service.

### Miracle Offering
Annual global/local missions giving campaign (November). Includes testimony videos, giving story videos, and offering video support across multiple Sundays.

### Sisterhood (Women's Ministry)
Women's ministry with two major events per year — **Spring** (April) and **Fall** (November). Each includes: graphics package, merch, KB slide, testimony/recap video, photo shoot, templates. Guest speakers brought in (e.g., Chrystal Evans Hurst - April 2025, Susie Larson - Nov 2025). Free admission, no livestream. Doors open early with worship, message, altar time, food, photobooths, and merch.

### Emmanuel Leadership College (ELC)
Leadership training program within ECC. Comms support includes video production, social media, graphics/design for events, announcements, and merch. Referenced frequently in the master calendar (ELC Graduation, ELC Launch Day, ELC Preview Day, ELC Prayer).

### Kids & Youth (NextGen)
Children's and student ministries. Events include Kids Camp, Emmanuel Youth Camp, and Next Gen Choir at Christmas. Referenced in comms calendar as occasional content producers or event recipients.

### Worship
Live worship team. Notable comms touchpoints: **Worship Nights** (require full video capture + production, graphics package, social coverage), music video production, album cover design.

### SHL Podcast (Shepherd-Hearted Leader)
Podcast led by (presumably) Pastor Nate Ruch. Comms handles: recording, editing, formatting, posting to YT/Spotify/Apple Music, thumbnail creation, social cuts and posting.

### Hispanic / Español Ministry
Spanish-language programming. Referenced in campus notes (SLP primarily). Comms implications not fully documented.

### Growth Track
Structured next-steps pathway for new attendees. Comms supports with announcement slides, video production (GrowthTrack video, headshots), and event support.

### Team Emmanuel
Volunteer program. Referenced in the master calendar (Team Emmanuel Conf in August — requires graphics package, supers, background, promo video, photobooth).

### Emmanuel LIVE
Online/streaming campus at live.emmanuelcc.org. Receives uploaded sermon video, podcast content, and serves as the digital location for remote attendees.

## Giving Structure

- **Tithe:** 10% baseline giving to local campus
- **Kingdom Builders:** Above-tithe giving to global/local missions
- **Miracle Offering:** Annual special offering (November)
- **Platforms:** Pushpay, ACH, credit card, crypto, stock, QCD

## Organizational Context for Comms

- **Scale:** 4 physical campuses, each with multiple services = large content surface area
- **Cadence:** High — 8–9 sermon series/year + 10–12 major event campaigns
- **Audience:** Multi-campus, multi-demographic; content must scale across all locations
- **Brand:** "Welcome Home" positioning — warm, accessible, community-focused
- **Key stakeholders:** Pastor Nate Ruch (priority decisions), Pastor Jodi Ruch (request originator)
