## Strategic Role — Online & Connections

Beyond comms requests, Jodi has been identified as a key person for two strategic conversations:

**1. Online broadcast strategy**
Nate described Jodi as the person most consistently burdened for the online campus alongside himself. She's an **implementer and systems thinker** — not just a dreamer. She'll ask: "What do we do with the information? Who's making follow-up calls?" She also has connections to volunteers currently serving online. Nate's direction to JC and Matt: *"Pull Jodi into a meeting — informally if possible — and explore what's feasible for online engagement now and long-term."* She managed a "Plan a Visit" pathway in a previous era (someone would meet first-time visitors at the door). She thinks in those terms naturally.

**2. Team Emmanuel / Connections**
Jodi leads the Team Emmanuel / Connections ministry. Nate sees her as the natural owner of the volunteer pipeline for online (chat hosts, prayer partners, online greeters). The comms team is *not* the team to recruit online prayer volunteers — that's Jodi's domain. The comms/production role is the tech layer; Jodi's role is the people layer.
